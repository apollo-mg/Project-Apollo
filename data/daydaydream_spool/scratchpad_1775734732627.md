# Epiphany Investigation: Silent Death of Prompt Worker Threads

## Initial Assessment
The epiphany suggests that ComfyUI prompt worker threads are crashing silently, leading to system hangs. This indicates a lack of supervision or a robust error-handling mechanism for concurrent tasks.

## Investigation Plan
1.  **Locate ComfyUI Core:** Find where the prompt processing logic resides.
2.  **Identify Threading Model:** Determine if it uses `threading`, `multiprocessing`, or `asyncio`.
3.  **Audit Error Handling:** Check for `try-except` blocks around worker loops and whether exceptions are logged or propagated.
4.  **Search for "Worker" or "Thread" patterns:** Look for the instantiation of worker threads.
5.  **Draft Proposal:** Suggest a supervisor/watchdog pattern or an async orchestration layer.

## Hypothesis
The "silent death" likely occurs in `src/agent/runner.ts` or `src/agent/pool.ts` where an asynchronous task or a worker is spawned without a robust `try-catch` or a supervisor that monitors the lifecycle of the task. If a promise rejects or an error is thrown in a way that isn't caught by the orchestrator, the worker might "vanish" from the perspective of the queue/pool.
