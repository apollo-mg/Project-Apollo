# Epiphany Investigation: Temporal Rhythm & Latency Optimization
## Objective
Investigate the latency in the 'Stream & Trigger' model, specifically the handoff between PipeWire/Whisper-v3-Turbo and the reasoning engine (DeepSeek-R1), to ensure the 'Voice Activity Detection' (VAD) acts as a natural breath rather than a technical delay.

## Initial Hypotheses
1. **VAD Latency:** The Voice Activity Detection mechanism might be waiting too long for silence to confirm the end of speech.
2. **Whisper-v3-Turbo Overhead:** The transcription process might be blocking the reasoning trigger.
3. **PipeWire Buffer Bloat:** Audio buffers in PipeWire might be introducing unnecessary latency before the stream reaches the transcription engine.
4. **Reasoning Trigger Lag:** The transition from "transcription complete" to "LLM prompt sent" might have an unoptimized middleware layer.

## Investigation Plan
1. Map the audio pipeline: PipeWire -> VAD -> Whisper -> LLM.
2. Locate the specific scripts/modules handling audio capture and transcription.
3. Audit the VAD parameters (silence threshold, duration).
4. Audit the Whisper-v3-Turbo implementation for streaming efficiency.
5. Check for any "wait" or "sleep" calls in the orchestration loop.
