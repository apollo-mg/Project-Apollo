# Pre-registration — is GGUF quantization damage concentrated on hesitation tokens, or just on uncertain positions?

**2026-09-24, before any KLD pass.** Source claim: Lotfi et al., "Quantized reasoning models"
(https://sanaelotfi.github.io/blog/quantized-reasoning-models/, shared by buun). Plan: `PLAN.md` (`fd38209`).

## Their claim, and the gap this tests

On R1-distills and QwQ-32B under GPTQ/AWQ/FlatQuant, per-token KL vs full precision is ~100x higher at
hesitation markers ("Wait", "But", "Alternatively") than at math tokens. They argue this drives overthinking at
low bits.

**The confound:** math tokens are low-entropy almost by construction, and hesitation markers sit at high-entropy
branch points. KL scales with entropy anyway. So the comparison that matters is **hesitation vs
non-hesitation positions at MATCHED reference entropy**. If the gap vanishes there, the mechanism is "quantization
hurts uncertain positions", and hesitation words are only where uncertainty lives.

**Prior art checked:** `ledger_precheck.py "KL divergence per token hesitation overthinking quantization" --deep`
-> no token-class-conditional KLD anywhere. Related:
- `lowbit-ladder/FINDING_DECAY_IS_LN4.md` (mean KLD vs bits);
- `viability/RESULT_EFFORT_SWEEP.md` (xhigh converts abstention to non-termination);
- INDEX L46 (thinking length as a confabulation signal).

**What this adds:** the first test of the claim on GGUF k-/i-quants with imatrix, and the entropy control the source
does not report.

## Corpus (frozen)

`corpus_reasoning.txt`, sha256 `7dcbf42bcc1adf0b840b50a227b25d235a9a844dd3d26e4e311c8ae245c687fa`: 215 deduplicated
reasoning traces (916,150 chars, ~229k tokens).
- Generated by **stock Qwen3.8-27B**, mostly at `xhigh`. Only sets whose filenames pin the stock model:
  `overthink_q6k`, `einstein_iq4` (Unsloth UD-IQ4_XS, hash in rows), `card_xhigh_rep*`, `dryrun_194_q6k_*`,
  `tiercal_27b`.
- Plain text (`Question: ...`, `Thinking: ...`). `llama-perplexity` tokenizes without parsing special tokens, so no
  chat-template tokens are used.
- Teacher-forced: every model scores the same text.

## Instrument

- buun `llama-perplexity` on `.194` P100s: `-c 2048 -b 512 -ub 512 -fa on`, f16 KV, identical flags every arm.
- **Reference: Qwen3.8-27B Q8_0** (29,047,086,048 B, the low-bit-ladder reference) writes the uint16 base. Every arm
  runs `--kl-divergence` with `TURBO_KLD_DUMP` for per-position KLD.
- Scored positions are the second half of each 2048 chunk.
- **Next token per position** comes from the token array stored in the base file.
- **Reference entropy per position** is computed from the base file's uint16 log-prob row.
- Chunks: as many full chunks as the corpus gives, capped by disk (target >= 96, about 98k scored positions).

**Arms** (one packager, Unsloth UD, except Q6_K): `UD-Q2_K_XL`, `UD-IQ3_XXS`, `UD-IQ4_XS`, `UD-Q4_K_M`, `Q6_K`.
`UD-IQ2_M` is optional and runs only if disk allows. Sizes are recorded from the files; bits are compared on scored
bytes (`METHOD_SCORED_BYTES`).

## Token classes (fixed now)

**HES:** the next token, decoded and stripped of leading whitespace, is exactly one of `Wait`, `But`, `Hmm`,
`Actually`, `Alternatively`, `However`, `Hold`, `Oh`, `Maybe`, `Perhaps`. Case-sensitive, capitalized forms only,
because the capital marks the start of a new thought. **OTHER** is every other position. A marker split across
several tokens counts only at its first token.

## Predictions

Unit of inference is the **chunk** (percentile bootstrap over chunks, 10,000 resamples); per-arm ratios use pooled
means within each resample.

| # | claim | test | conf |
|---|---|---|---:|
| H1 | Raw concentration exists | mean KLD(HES) / mean KLD(OTHER) > 3 in **every** arm, CI lower bound > 1 | 0.75 |
| H2 | **It survives entropy matching** | Within reference-entropy deciles (defined on all positions), pooled HES/OTHER KLD ratio (decile-weighted by HES count) > 1.5 in the two lowest-bit arms, CI lower bound > 1 | 0.35 |
| H3 | It grows as bits fall | The raw HES/OTHER ratio at UD-Q2_K_XL > ratio at Q6_K (bootstrap difference CI excludes 0) | 0.55 |
| H4 | Hesitation KLD separates quants more than mean KLD | ratio of HES-KLD (Q2_K_XL / Q6_K) > ratio of mean KLD (Q2_K_XL / Q6_K) | 0.50 |

**Reading, fixed now:**
- **H1 true, H2 false:** the effect is entropy, not hesitation. The fix is "protect uncertain positions"
  (sampling or KV at branch points), not "penalize hesitation words".
- **H1 and H2 both true:** hesitation tokens are specifically fragile under quantization, and a marker-targeted logit
  penalty (plan step 2) becomes worth testing.
- **H1 false:** the source's effect does not transfer to imatrix GGUF quants.

**Descriptive:** KLD vs entropy curves per class; top-1 agreement at HES positions; per-marker breakdown.
