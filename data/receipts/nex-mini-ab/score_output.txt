=== Is NEX's decode rate just an answer-length artefact? ===
NEX's answers are ~8x shorter, and decode slows as the KV grows, so the cross-arm rate
could be a length effect rather than a model property. Checked two ways:
  NEX_R1     by length quartile: 47.9@136tok  47.9@225tok  47.9@364tok  47.9@952tok   |  200-400 tok only: 47.94 (n=183)
  QWEN_R1    by length quartile: 42.9@1247tok  42.9@2001tok  42.8@2714tok  42.6@3914tok   |  200-400 tok only: 43.06 (n=4)
  ORNITH_R2  by length quartile: 42.8@266tok  42.8@523tok  42.8@757tok  42.7@1330tok   |  200-400 tok only: 42.83 (n=85)
  NEX_R2     by length quartile: 48.1@135tok  48.1@221tok  48.1@363tok  48.0@866tok   |  200-400 tok only: 48.11 (n=168)
  The within-arm slope is real but tiny (QWEN 42.9 -> 42.6 t/s across 1247 -> 3914 tokens),
  and the length-matched rates reproduce the full gap. NEX's edge is not a length artefact.
