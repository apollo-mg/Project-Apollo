**Structural notes:**

| Layer | Contents | Z-order |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Background gradient, clouds, motion lines | Back |
| 2 | Bicycle (wheels, frame, cranks, handlebars) | Mid |
| 3 | Pelican (legs → body → wing → neck → head → beak) | Front |

**Key design decisions:**

1. **Pelican silhouette** — The oversized lower pouch (`Q 295 152` control point) creates the instantly recognizable bill shape rather than a generic "big beak."
2. **S-curve neck** — A quadratic Bézier with 22px stroke width avoids a separate head-circle disconnect; reads as a continuous anatomical form.
3. **Riding posture** — Body ellipse rotated −8°, legs articulated to separate pedal positions (top-left / bottom-right of crank), conveying mid-stride motion.
4. **Frame geometry** — Diamond frame with visible chainstays, seatstays, down-tube, and fork; red (`#c0392b`) against the white bird for maximum contrast.

**Verification:**
- Open in any browser — renders without JS, no external dependencies.
- Scales cleanly; all geometry is vector, no raster fallbacks.
- Spoke groups use rotational transforms (rotate 22.5°) for 8-spoke symmetry without 8 manual line elements.
