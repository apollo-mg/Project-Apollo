## Faults

1. **No wings/arms on the handlebars** – the pelican is just sitting on the bike; nothing reaches forward to grip the bars, so it doesn't read as "riding."
2. **Tail is a jagged polygon** – straight-line facets look angular rather than feather-like.
3. **Eye too small** (r = 3.5) – barely visible in the render; no highlight.
4. **Beak slightly over-long** (tip at x = 385) – pushes the head/beak cluster toward the front wheel and makes the bird look top-heavy.
5. **Neck volume is only a thin filled sliver** – the thick white stroke and the thin filled quad don't align well, leaving a visible seam at the head junction.
