Looking at the rendering, the scene is recognisably a pelican on a bicycle, but a few things hold it back:

**Faults**
1. The beak/pouch is very long relative to the body, making the bird look lopsided and stealing visual weight from the bike.
2. The near wing (the "arm" reaching to the handlebars) is a thin 14-px stroke with no fill, so it reads as a faint line rather than a solid wing.
3. The tail feathers are tiny and almost invisible in the render.
4. The body ellipse is a bit small for the 48-px wheels, making the rider look undersized.
