## Faults

1. **Body ellipse oversized** (rx 55, ry 65) – makes the pelican a giant blob dwarfing the bike.
2. **Wing is a large filled path** (`M195 175 Q250 185 316 160 … Z`) that merges with the body into one amorphous white mass instead of a thin limb reaching the bars.
3. **Head too small** (r 26) relative to the body, and the neck is a sliver, so the bird has no clear "neck + head" silhouette.
4. **Legs are short and start well inside the body ellipse**, so they are nearly hidden; the pelican doesn't read as *sitting on* the saddle with legs reaching the pedals.
5. **Beak/pouch extends past x = 305**, well past the head-tube of the frame, making the bird look like it's poking through the bike.
