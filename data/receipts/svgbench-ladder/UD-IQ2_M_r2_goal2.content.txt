Looking at the ASCII rendering, the image is recognizable as a pelican on a bicycle, but I see a few faults:

**Faults:**
1. The chain line is at y=304 while the wheel/crank hubs are at y=310 — it floats above the axis.
2. The throat pouch is too small and flat; a pelican's gular pouch is a major, sagging feature.
3. The legs are single straight strokes with no knee bend, making them look stick-like.
4. The handlebar is an ambiguous curl rather than a clear bar shape.
5. The neck outline (thin stroke) is barely visible next to the thick white neck fill and doesn't fully cap the ends, leaving a slight gap at the head junction.
