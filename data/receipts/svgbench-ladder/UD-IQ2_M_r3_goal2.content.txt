## Faults

1. **Beak is far too long** – it stretches ~127 px to the right, nearly reaching the front wheel, making the pelican look lumpy and the upper half of the image dominated by a horizontal spike.
2. **Beak pouch is oversized** – the cubic curve dips to y≈170, creating a large triangular blob that reads as a separate shape rather than part of the beak.
3. **Wing/arm is a thin, very long line** – the stroke path from (380,190)→(518,240) renders as a nearly-horizontal hairline that is hard to read as a wing.
4. **Pelican floats above the frame** – the body sits high and the single thin leg diagonal is barely visible in the density map, so the bird looks disconnected from the seat.
5. **Leg/foot barely register** – a 7-px-wide stroke over 135 px of vertical travel vanishes in the 64×30 grid; the webbed-foot ellipse is almost invisible.
