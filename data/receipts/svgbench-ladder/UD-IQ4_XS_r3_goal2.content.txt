Looking at the ASCII rendering, I can identify these faults:

1. **Neck is nearly invisible** – it blends into the body and head; no distinct S-curve reads between the blob body and the head.
2. **Beak/pouch merge with the head** – the upper beak, pouch, and head form one amorphous mass in the top-right; the pouch does not clearly hang *below* the beak line.
3. **Wing-to-handlebar is muddy** – the wing shape overlaps the body so much that the "reaching to the bars" gesture is lost; it just looks like the body extends rightward.
4. **Legs/pedals are ambiguous** – the leg strokes overlap the frame tubes and crank, making it hard to tell where the pelican's legs end and the bicycle hardware begins.
5. **Tail is too small** – a few pixels of light grey that don't register as a tail.
