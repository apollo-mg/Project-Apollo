## Faults

1. **Scene is essentially invisible** – at the 64×30 sampling grid the only surviving detail is a few faint `%` dots where the thin wheel-spoke lines fall. The pelican body (near-white on light blue), the thin red frame, and the beak all average into the sky and produce no distinct cells.
2. **Insufficient stroke weight / element size** – the 8 px frame, 22 px neck, and 65 px-radius wheels are all sub-cell at this resolution; nothing registers as a separate tonal region.
3. **Low contrast palette** – the pelican's `#fafaf5` against `#a8d8f0` gives very little luminance separation, so the bird dissolves into the background.
4. **Wheels read as dots, not wheels** – the 9 px ring plus 2.5 px inner ring collapse to one or two cells at best.

| Wheels collapsed to dots | Ring thickened to 14 px; spoke lines kept; hub enlarged to r 9 |
| Beak / pouch small | Beak now spans x 480→630 (≈12 cells wide); pouch path enlarged |
| Ground too pale | Darker green `#3E7B27` + 8 px black road strip → distinct bottom rows |
| Overall scale | Pelican body rx 70→82, head r 28→34, legs/feet enlarged so each feature covers ≥2 grid cells |
