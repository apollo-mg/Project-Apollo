## Assessment

The rendering is recognizable as a pelican on a bicycle, but a few issues stand out:

1. **Legs too thin / unclear** – the 9 px orange lines read as vague strokes rather than distinct bird legs; the feet don't visually "grip" the pedals.
2. **Body slightly small** – the white ellipse (144 × 96 px) is dwarfed by the 124 px wheels, making the pelican look perched rather than firmly riding.
3. **Tail nearly invisible** – the tiny triangle on the left is lost in the render.
4. **Wing shape** – the long tapering shape toward the handlebars looks more like an arm than a folded wing; a bit more feathering would help.
5. **Beak pouch** – the lower beak is a simple triangle; a pelican's gular pouch should bulge more.
