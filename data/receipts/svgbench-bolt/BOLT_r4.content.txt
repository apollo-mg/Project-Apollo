### Design Details:
*   **The Pelican:** I used a large yellow path for the beak to capture the pelican's most distinctive feature. The body is a light grey/white ellipse with a curved line to suggest a wing.
*   **The Bicycle:** The bike is constructed from basic geometric primitives (circles for wheels, lines for the frame) to keep the SVG lightweight and scalable.
*   **Composition:** The pelican is positioned centrally over the bike frame, with its "legs" positioned near the pedals to create the illusion of riding.
