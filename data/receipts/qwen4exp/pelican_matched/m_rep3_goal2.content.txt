Looking at the rendering, the composition is mostly recognizable but has these faults:

1. **Pouch shape is too flat** – the pelican's most distinctive feature (the large throat pouch) reads more like a second flat beak rather than a scoop/bucket hanging below.
2. **Feet don't align with pedals** – the leg paths end slightly above/beside the crank, so it doesn't clearly read as "riding."
3. **Wing/handlebar contact is ambiguous** – the wing shape is too narrow and doesn't visually connect to the handlebar curve.
4. **Tail feathers are too small and spiky** – they read as noise rather than a fanned tail.
5. **Neck-to-head transition is abrupt** – the head circle sits slightly disconnected from the neck path.
