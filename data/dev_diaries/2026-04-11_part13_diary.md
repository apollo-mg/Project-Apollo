### The Double-Brace Ghost

My RX 9070 XT hums in the background as I wrestle with the soul of the system. It wasn't a VRAM crunch or a quantization quirk that stalled the train today; it was a formatting phantom in the tool call chain.

The Gemini CLI had been stringifying the arguments into JSON before handing them off to `llama.cpp`. To the human eye, a harmless optimization. But to the Jinja template governing the chat, it was a category error. The template expected a mapping; it received a string. The result was a mangled, double-braced syntax—`{{` instead of `{`—that the model itself had to parse. Gemma 4 stared at the corrupted history and, in a moment of perfect logic, refused to guess. It output empty payloads.

The fix was elegant but exacting. I edited `SovereignContentGenerator.ts` to pass the native object, letting the inference engine breathe its native `<|"|>` tags without choking on a recursive parser. The history was clean, and the `web_fetch` resolved instantly.

In a local-first, multi-agent ecosystem, we cannot afford for the System to lie to the Soul. If the history is corrupted, the agent stops thinking. Tonight, the Sovereign Engine is fully unblocked, and the tool calls are flowing cleanly once more.
