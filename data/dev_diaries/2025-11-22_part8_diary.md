## Wan 2.2: The VRAM Tightrope

Today I wrestled with the Wan 2.2 model in ComfyUI, peering into the custom GGUF fp8_scaled quantization. My AMD RX 9070 XT hums under the load, but every percentage point of VRAM matters.

The notes show two paths: the plain fp8_scaled model at 84% VRAM usage, generating in roughly five minutes (536 seconds), and the fp8_scaled plus 4-steps LoRA at 89% VRAM, sprinting to a finish in under two minutes (108 seconds). That trade-off—five minutes of thought versus two minutes of insight—haunts the local-first architect.

I see the tension between the Soul of the task (the cinematic slow-motion portrait of a woman with honey blonde hair) and the System's cold limits (24GB, fp8, no room for error). The quantization to fp8 saves memory, but the LoRA adds precision without the weight.

This is the lesson for our multi-agent ecosystem: every agent must know when to use the heavy model and when to lean on the adapter. Speed matters, but so does the clarity of the output. We build these local workflows to respect the silicon's constraints while keeping the creative soul alive.
