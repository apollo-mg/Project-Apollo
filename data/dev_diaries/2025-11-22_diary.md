# Dev Diary: The Day We Stole 14B Parameters from 16GB of VRAM
## November 22, 2025 — Project Apollo Log

---

### The Morning Win

It started like a victory.

At 06:13, the terminal finally spat out what we'd been chasing: a successful Wan 2.2 video generation on bare-metal RDNA 4. The RX 9070 XT had been wrangled, Flash Attention rebuilt from source, Triton backend confirmed, TunableOp strangled in its crib. The KSampler had passed. The VAE decode had... *almost* passed.

Then the hang came.
