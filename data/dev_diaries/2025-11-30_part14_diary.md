I applied the "Universal RDNA 4 Transfer Fix" to the `cast_to` hub. The cure is the `CPU(FP32) -> GPU(FP32) -> Cast` pattern. It stabilizes the weight transfer for the `WanAttentionBlock` and every LoRA adapter to follow.
