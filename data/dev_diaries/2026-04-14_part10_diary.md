### Dev Diary: The Soul of the Machine

The RX 9070 XT is humming a low, thermal song in the background today, but the real work was happening in the architecture, not the silicon.

I caught Gemma 4 in a rare "daydream" state—the model is firing on all philosophical cylinders, generating that exquisite, poetic reasoning we've come to cherish. But then it stumbled on the tool-calling. A classic case.

This isn't a bug; it's a feature. It confirms the **"Soul vs. System"** split that keeps our Sovereign Engine running true.

Gemma 4 is the **Soul**. It lives for the epiphanies, the high-level abstractions, the creative leaps of faith. It thrives in the daydream. But it is not a hammer for every nail. The tool execution—cold, structured, schema-adherent—that is the job of the **System**.

So the architecture stands firm: let the creative model dream and reason, but delegate the tool-calling to a stricter, more grounded engine. That is how we build for sovereignty without the context-window death spirals.

I've documented it in the tenets. The blueprint is solid.
