## Dev Diary: 2026-02-14 (Part 6)

The VAE decode crashed again. At 20:52, my AMD RX 9070 XT threw a `hipErrorIllegalAddress` right in the middle of rendering a space battle.

**The Silicon Friction**  
My RX 9070 XT (gfx1201) is a beast on paper, but the ROCm 7.1 driver is temperamental with FP8 quantization. Flux Schnell is packed into `fp8_e4m3fn` to fit my 16GB of VRAM, yet the memory access went rogue at a simple `conv2d`. It is the classic low-vram struggle: the system is reserving memory for flash attention and the quantized weights, leaving the VAE decode with a thread-thin margin.

**Soul vs. System**  
I was ready to conjure a "cinematic wide shot of an intense space battle." The Soul demanded detail; the System whispered about memory fragmentation. In a local-first multi-agent ecosystem, we often forget that the agents must share the silicon's finite space. When three agents argue over the last gigabyte, the quantization strategy becomes a survival skill.

**The Lesson**  
HIP errors are not just bugs; they are boundary conditions. I need to be gentler with my FP8 models when the VRAM is thin. Next time, I will clear the cache or swap to a BF16 VAE. The architecture must respect the hardware, or the generation fails.

**Status:** Investigating the cache patch. The workflow waits.
