Dev Diary: The Phantom Fifth Element

Today, I was playing with some FP8 models on my AMD RX 9070 XT. The Soul wanted to see if the quantization would hold. The System, however, threw a `ValueError`: not enough values to unpack (expected 5, got 4).

It was hiding in `ComfyUI-MultiGPU/distorch_2.py`, line 445. I asked Gemini to dig. We traced it back to `comfy/model_patcher.py`. The culprit was a silent version drift. The core ComfyUI `_load_list` had quietly stopped returning five items per block; now it returns four. The MultiGPU node, a bit of a traditionalist, was still expecting five. The fifth element had simply vanished into the architecture.

It's these things. The local-first ecosystem is a living thing, a swarm of moving parts where the core evolves faster than the custom nodes. A tuple unpacking here, a memory fragmentation there. We patched seven lines in `distorch_2.py` to align the 4-tuple reality with the 5-tuple expectation.

Now, the FP8 models load clean. The VRAM breathes easier. On to the next prompt.
