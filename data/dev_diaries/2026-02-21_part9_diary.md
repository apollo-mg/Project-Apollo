# Dev Diary: The Voice Bridge (2026-02-21)

The friction between the "Soul" (my goal of a seamless, intelligent partner) and the "System" (the stubborn, finicky Linux audio architecture) reached a boiling point today. I spent most of the evening wrestling with PipeWire and shell syntax, but we finally broke through the barrier. Jarvis is no longer just a text-box in a terminal; he’s starting to develop a voice.

### The Hierarchy of Truth
I spent a good chunk of the session mapping out the decision-making architecture for the "Unified Tool Interception" (UTI). It's a hierarchical logic gate. 
- **Local Hardware** (the "Source of Truth") is checked first—immediate and hard-coded.
- **The Vault** (my local shop knowledge) is the specialist path.
- **The Web** (SearXNG/Jina) is the research path for when the model hits a knowledge gap.
- **The Weights** are the last resort for general logic.

This hierarchy is critical. I don't want a general LLM hallucinating my shop's hardware specs when I have a perfectly good PDF in my `/vault`.

### The Sound of Sovereignty
The real breakthrough was the audio bridge. Using Tailscale, I realized I could treat my Android phone as a remote microphone for my Ubuntu machine. 

The technical hurdle was the "Virtual Audio Cable." I attempted to use `pw-loopback` to create a virtual node, but the shell syntax for multi-line commands in my script was causing a nightmare of parsing errors. I had to scrap the multi-line logic and rewrite the script as a clean, single-line PipeWire command to bypass the shell's interpretation quirks. 

Now, I have a `Jarvis_Virtual_Mic` node in the PipeWire graph. Using `qpwgraph`, I can literally "patch" my desk mics and my phone's audio stream (via AudioRelay) into a single input. It's a real-world, hardware-level audio mixer.

### The Infrastructure of the Foundry
The "Foundry" is no longer just a concept. The logging pipeline (`foundry_logs.jsonl`) is active. We are capturing every interaction, every trace. This is the data that will eventually train my custom models—true model sovereignty. 

### Lessons Learned & The Road Ahead
- **Hardware is the Anchor:** My RDNA 4 architecture is holding up, but the VRAM management between the `jarvis.py` core and the new voice bridge is something I need to watch.
- **The Web is Now High-Fidelity:** Integrating SearXNG and Jina Reader has moved us from simple web scraping to actual deep research. The "Research Path" is now a legitimate, high-fidelity intelligence source.
- **Next Step:** The voice bridge is operational. The next task is the "Audio-to-Speech" latency. If I can get the latency down to sub-500ms, we've officially achieved "Live" parity with the commercial-grade AI models, but on my own hardware.

**Status:** *Phase 6 (The Architect) is moving fast. The foundation is laid. Jarvis is listening.*
