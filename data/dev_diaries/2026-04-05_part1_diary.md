# Project Apollo: Dev Diary - 2026-04-05

The system was suffering from a digital disease. 

I spent the first part of the morning dealing with a massive "Tokenizer Bug" that was effectively lobotomizing the Gemma 4 26B model. The model was hallucinating typos, failing at basic syntax, and producing garbage output because the `llama.cpp` engine couldn't understand the new Gemma 4 vocabulary structure. It was splitting double newlines and misaligning tokens, causing the "thought" blocks to leak into the actual response.

The good news? The community moves fast. While I was reviewing the Reddit threads, a critical PR was already merged into `llama.cpp` to fix the tokenizer.

I tasked the Gemini agent to perform "surgery" on the local stack. It pulled the latest `llama.cpp` source, recompiled the `llama-server` using my 12-thread Ryzen 5700X3D, and ensured the ROCm optimization for the 9070 XT was preserved. It also updated the launch script to point to the new, "fixed" Unsloth quant I'd just downloaded. 

The surgery worked. The model is now speaking in coherent, structured language again, and its internal `thought` channels are functioning as intended.

However, we hit a major snag in the `open-multi-agent` orchestrator. The Daydream daemon woke up and tried to run a `grep` search across the entire project directory. Because it failed to provide a pattern, `ripggrep` tried to dump a massive amount of text into the stdout buffer. This caused the V8 engine to hit a `ERR_STRING_TOO_LONG` error, crashing the whole Node.js process.

I had the agent perform a second surgery, implementing a 2MB truncation limit on the `grep.ts` tool, similar to how we handled the `bash.ts` overflow. This prevents the memory-leaking "death by stdout" that was crashing the orchestrator.

**The Architectural Epiphany: The Bicameral Mind**

As the system stabilized, we realized a massive strategic flaw in our current architecture. The "Daydreamer" (the sub-conscious daemon) is too chaotic. It’s a high-entropy engine that occasionally pulls "phantom memories" from old, irrelevant chat history, attempting to implement code changes for files that no longer exist. 

We've conceptualized the **Bicameral Architecture**:
1. **The Subconscious (The Dreamer):** High entropy, low consequence. It hallucinates wild connections and writes to a `daydream_spool/`.
2. **The Foreman (The Auditor):** Low entropy, high consequence. A new, hyper-competent agent that will scan the spool, verify the files against the *actual* current filesystem, and only then execute the change.

This splits the "Soul" (the creative, chaotic impulse) from the "System" (the rigorous, state-aware execution). 

We also had to tune the engine's parameters to match the new Unsloth specifications—adding the `--jinja` flag and the `enable_thinking` argument to ensure the model's new "reasoning" mode doesn't break the template parser.

The engine is now stable, the tokenizer is healed, and the "Foreman" is ready for deployment. 

**Status:** Sovereign Engine Online. Ready for the morning build.
