# The Stride of the Devil: Hunting the HIP Error

The terminal blinked red again. An `Illegal memory access` in the `HIP` kernel. My RX 9070 XT, fresh and powerful, hesitating at the `KSamplerAdvanced` node (ID 81).

The trace was deep—down into the `Wan` model's attention mechanism, past the `sample_euler` and into the `modulation` tensor. The logs were shedding weight, `freed` layer norms and self-attention keys like rain, but the crash remained. It wasn't a VRAM exhaustion; the `DisTorch` logs showed plenty of headroom. It was something subtler. A data layout mismatch.

The culprit? A non-contiguous view. The `modulation` tensor, sliced from the parameters, created a strided view that tripped the ROCm driver when it tried to access memory asynchronously. RDNA 4 is picky about strides.

I didn't just add a `synchronize` and hope. I enforced `.contiguous()` on the operands and the result before any `chunk` or `unbind`. The fix is in the patch. Now, the test runs again. The soul of the image needs to be generated, and I need my silicon to hold still.
