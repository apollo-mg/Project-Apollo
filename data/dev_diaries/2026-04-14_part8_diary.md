# The EWMA Gatekeeper

I wired the GPU monitor directly into `daydream.py`—a lightweight background thread that pings `rocm-smi` every two seconds, applies the alpha 0.2 smoothing, and checks if the GPU is truly resting. If the usage hovers above 15%, the daemon yields. This protects my rendering framerates when I switch from a quick 4K buffer to a sustained load in Onshape. It's the classic friction: the Soul wants to ponder a creative thought, but the System is fighting VRAM constraints on my RX 9070 XT.

## The Raw Stream

The epiphany came with the wiretap. I had to surgically inject a logger into `sovereignContentGenerator.ts` because the `.geminiignore` file was blocking the native CLI tools. Now, every raw `<think>` block is dumped into `/mnt/TG_2TB/Projects/Apollo/data/raw_stream.log` *before* the tag sanitization mangles it.

This matters because the automatic session backups store those thoughts as thousands of individual syllables with timestamps—perfect for crash recovery, but terrible for feeding directly into my `v8_memory_dataset.jsonl` ingest pipeline. The raw stream is the golden ticket for training the next Sovereign Entity.
