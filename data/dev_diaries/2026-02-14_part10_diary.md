Apollo Dev Diary - 2026-02-14 (Part 10 of 49)

The Spark

It was the space battle again. I had the cinematic wide shot in my head—battle-worn starfighters, green laser fire streaking across the swirling clouds of a gas giant—but the machine was not cooperating.

At 20:52:09, ComfyUI choked. Node 8, VAEDecode. The stack trace was a vertical climb through F.conv2d, up the upsample chain, and into the abyss of a torch.AcceleratorError. Specifically, a HIP error: an illegal memory access was encountered.

The Friction

My RX 9070 XT (gfx1201, 16GB VRAM) is a beast, but Project Apollo is running with its head down in LOW_VRAM mode. Flash attention is active. The Flux model (Flux1-schnell-fp8) was loaded as torch.float8_e4m3fn, then manually cast to torch.bfloat16. Everything was fine until the VAE tried to decode the latent into pixels.

The System is cold. It demands every byte be accounted for when the multi-agent swarms multiply. The Soul of this project is local-first intelligence—no clouds, no walled gardens, just silicon and prompt. But the memory access went sideways at the upsample, suggesting memory fragmentation in the ComfyUI-MultiGPU patches or a ROCm 7.1.1 quirk I haven't tamed.

The Architecture

I see two paths. I could pass AMD_SERIALIZE_KERNEL=3 to let the kernel serialize and see if the illegal address reveals itself. Or I could compile with TORCH_USE_HIP_DSA to enable device-side assertions and catch the stride early. The logs are hinting at a clash between the FP8 quantization and the lowvram patches: when the VAE unloads to CPU and reloads, does it leave a ghost in the machine?

The Lesson

The lesson is that quantization strategies are not free. An FP8 model saves VRAM, yes, but when paired with flash attention and a 2GB reserve, the decode becomes the bottleneck. For the local-first multi-agent ecosystem, efficient memory management is not a luxury—it is the oxygen that lets agents breathe while they debate the nature of gas giants.

For now, I am letting the kernel serialize. The battle ships wait.
