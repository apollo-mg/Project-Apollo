### The Silicon: RDNA 4 and the "TunableOp" Crisis

The biggest technical hurdle today was the interaction between the new PyTorch 2.4.0 build and the AMD RX 9070 XT (GFX1201). We hit a wall with the standard `TunableOp` implementation—it was failing to capture the specific GEMM shapes for the Qwen 3.5 architecture, likely due to a race condition in the ROCm 7.2 profiling layer. 

The solution was pure, unadulterly "Sovereign" engineering: I let the secondary Gemini instance write a custom tuning script (`pure_pytorch_tune.py`). It bypasses the broken upstream logic by manually mocking the high-load tensors (the 4096 sequence lengths) and iterating through the shapes itself. 

Watching the GPU power draw hit 243W during the calibration was visceral. It’s a heavy, hot, and necessary "Deep Dream" phase. We are currently waiting for the `vllm_qwen9b_tunableop.csv` to write to disk, which only happens once the custom loop finishes its work.
