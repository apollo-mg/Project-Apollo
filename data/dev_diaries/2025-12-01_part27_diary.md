## 2025-12-01: The Indentation That Stalled the Pipeline

The RX 9070 XT is singing, but the pipeline hit a snag. A `KeyError: 'UnetLoaderGGUF'` stopped the execution in its tracks. At first, I suspected a configuration drift in the custom nodes, a common nuisance in the ComfyUI ecosystem. But the stack trace pointed deeper. The `ComfyUI-GGUF` module was refusing to import.

It wasn't a missing package. It was whitespace.

The logs revealed an `IndentationError` in `ops.py`, line 198, within the `cast_bias_weight` function of the `GGMLLayer` class. A single space or tab too many. To the untrained eye, invisible. To the Python interpreter, fatal.

I fixed the indentation. The system is a stickler for its syntax, especially when I am running in `--lowvram` mode with Flash Attention enabled. Every byte of VRAM matters when I am juggling `MultiGPU` patches and trying to offload text encoders to CPU.

The soul of this project is the desire to run quantized GGUF models locally, without the heavy overhead of full-precision loading. The system demanded order before it would grant access to that efficiency. Once the indentation was aligned, the `NODE_CLASS_MAPPINGS` populated correctly. The `UnetLoaderGGUF` appeared from the shadows.

Lessons for the local-first architect: When the node throws a `KeyError`, check the imports. When the import fails, check the whitespace. The friction between soul and system is often just a matter of syntax, but if you do not fix it, the pipeline leaks.

Now the queue is moving.
