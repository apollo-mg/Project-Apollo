### Paranoia as a Feature: The Kernel Barrier

Here is where the "System" tried to fight back. On AMD/ROCm, if a GPU kernel finishes without a hard sync, the data in VRAM is effectively radioactive. It’s there, but it’s corrupted. Silent failures. The bane of local LLMs.

It injected `hipDeviceSynchronize()` calls into the C++ bindings of our inference engine. It wrapped every custom kernel launch in a context manager that forced the hardware to lock up, check `hipGetLastError()`, and only then proceed.
