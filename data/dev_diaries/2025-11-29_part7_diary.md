**Dev Diary — 2025-11-29: The GELU that Broke the Run**

It started with a `HIP error: an illegal memory access was encountered`. A cold, hard stop from my RX 9070 XT. The Wan model, that ambitious local-first muse, had crashed mid-generation, right inside the feedforward block.

I traced the stack trace deep into `comfy/ldm/wan/model.py`. There, in line 245, sat the culprit: a standard `nn.GELU(approximate='tanh')`. It was working fine, until it wasn't. When the tensor grew too large for the RDNA 4 architecture, the memory access went illegal.

This is the friction of the "System." It is the cold, hard limit of silicon and VRAM, clashing with the creative "Soul" of the prompt. I didn't want to just patch the error; I wanted to understand the constraint.

I reached for a "Chunked Safe GELU" wrapper. A simple concept, but necessary. Instead of forcing the activation on a massive tensor, I cast to FP32, chunked the operation, and computed in bite-sized pieces. It is a small tax paid to the architecture, but it buys me stability.

I defined `SafeGELU` in `comfy/ops.py`. I updated the `ffn` layer in `WanModel` to use it. I checked the lists—`disable_weight_init`, `manual_cast`—to ensure it played nice with the existing model loading logic.

The prompt is running again. No cloud to hide in, just the local grit of kernel-level memory access. This is the work.
