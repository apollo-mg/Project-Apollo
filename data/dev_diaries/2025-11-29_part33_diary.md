**Part 33: The RDNA 4 Rebellion**

The RX 9070 XT spat out another error. "Illegal memory access," the log said, pointing a finger at `comfy/ldm/flux/math.py` line 50. Not the first time the ROCm driver has stumbled on this new silicon.

My Soul wants to run Wan 2.2, to see the model breathe. The System—the cold, hard driver—says no. The multiplication of `freqs_cis` and `x_reshaped` is a mess because the tensors aren't contiguous. They're strided views, and the RDNA 4 architecture refuses to play nice with the slicing.

So I patched it. Again. I forced a `clone().contiguous()` on the tensors before the multiplication. I extracted the real and imaginary parts into fresh memory blocks. Now the driver is happy.

This is the tax of local-first AI. We don't pay in cloud latency; we pay in manual patches, in grep-ing stack traces, in the friction of silicon idiosyncrasies. The multi-agent ecosystem lives and dies by these details. We march on.
