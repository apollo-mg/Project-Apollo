**Dev Diary: 2025-11-20 | The Memory Dance**

The afternoon found me wrestling with a HIP illegal memory access error on my AMD RX 9070 XT. A classic silicon tantrum between the Soul (my Flux FP8 generation) and the System (the cold, hard limits of VRAM and TunableOp).

The auto-tuner, bless its heart, keeps picking kernels that look fast but crash on RDNA 4. I disabled it—safe mode—and the generation hummed. But that 60% speedup? Too precious to lose. I re-enabled it. The occasional crash on a first run is a fair price for velocity.

Then, the shift to video. Wan 2.2 was too greedy for 16GB. LTX-Video is light, but HunyuanVideo (GGUF quantized) promised the quality I crave without the weight. I installed the Manager, swapped the loader for a GGUF version. But 73 frames? Too ambitious. The GPU hung, silently calculating 29 million latents.

I cut it to 33 frames. The GPU woke up. `rocm-smi` showed the bars climbing.

In local-first AI, every fragment of VRAM counts. Quantization is not just a trick; it is the bridge between massive models and consumer silicon. And TunableOp? Keep it on. The speed is worth the occasional stumble.

Now, watching the progress bar, I wonder: what is the next multi-agent dance when these agents all need to breathe at once?
