## The Illegal Memory Access

I was pushing Wan 2.1 14B FP8 through ComfyUI on my RX 9070 XT. Gemini and I watched as SDMA was disabled and the model loaded in lowvram mode—131 patches sliding into place. Then, at the KSampler node, the kernel crashed. Not a VRAM shortage; the system reported gigabytes to spare. It was an "Illegal Memory Access" at the RMSNorm, specifically at `torch.mean(chunk**2, ...)`.

The stack trace was a rabbit hole of nested calls—patcher extensions, samplers, and model bases—until I found the culprit in the mean calculation. It felt like the System (cold, hard ROCm) was rejecting the Soul (my creative FP8 inference).

I learned that quantization strategies can be brittle when the driver quirks pile up. For the local-first multi-agent ecosystem to thrive, the friction between the silicon's limits and the model's dreams must be smoothed. Next, I will try the AMD serialization flag and re-enable SDMA. The math must add up, or the inference stays silent.
