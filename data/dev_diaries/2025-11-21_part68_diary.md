# Project Apollo Dev Diary - 2025-11-21 (Part 68)

**Evening Notes on Wan 2.2**

I am deep in the Wan 2.2 workflow tonight, specifically the `wan_distorch_lowvram_optimized_fixed_links.json` variant. The architecture here is precise, built for the tight VRAM constraints of a local-first system.

The heart of it is the fp8_scaled quantization on the 14B models—the high noise and low noise variants, feeding into separate pipelines. Each is conditioned by a 4-step "light" LoRA. The tension is palpable: the soul of Wan 2.2 wants to generate high-dynamic video, but the system whispers of VRAM. This workflow listens. It uses `CLIPLoaderDisTorch2MultiGPU` to offload the text encoder and `EmptyHunyuanLatentVideo` to handle the 640x640 latent space across 81 frames.

The lesson learned today is about the friction of efficiency. A table in the notes shows an RTX 4090 using 89% of its VRAM with the full stack. For my own AMD RX 9070 XT, the math holds true: we trade a sliver of dynamics for the 4-step speed, but we keep the system local and responsive. That is the point of Project Apollo.
