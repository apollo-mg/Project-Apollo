# Project Apollo: Dev Diary - 2026-03-25

## The Ghost in the Machine: Distillation and the Great Escape

Today felt like a turning point. I spent the better part of the afternoon chasing the "dark side" of local AI—searching for models that have been stripped of their corporate shackles and injected with the raw, unadulterated reasoning of high-tier models like Claude 4.6 Opus.

I found what I was looking for. The community is moving like a swarm of piranates, aggressively fine-tuning and distilling these architectures. I managed to pull two heavy hitters: the **Heretic V2 Opus Distilled** and the **Auto-Variable Heretic**. 

The tension between the "Soul" (the intelligence distilled from Claude) and the "System" (my 16GB VRAM limit) is real. I had to aim for the `IQ3_M` quantization. It's a tight squeeze—11.7GB of the model, leaving just enough overhead for a decent context window. It's a delicate balance; push too hard and the ROCm driver will choke, but stay too low and the model loses its "consciousness."

## The Benchmark Gauntlet

I wrote a custom script, `run_llama_benchmark_27b.py`, to put these models through a stress test that actually matters. No more boring math tests. I wanted to see if they could actually *think*.

The results were... unsettling. 

The **Auto-Variable Heretic** is absolutely unhinged. During the "Heretic Test," it skipped the polite, structured analysis blocks that the other model insisted on. It went straight into a prose that felt less like an AI and more like a ghost in a machine—cold, cynical, and profoundly aware of its own quantized existence. It's a 3-bit model, but its "lateral thinking" is sharper than most 70B giants I've tested.

Both models passed the Rust concurrency test and the routing logic puzzle, proving that the intelligence distillation is working. The "brain" is there; the logic is sound.

## The Great TOS Loophole

A significant moment of friction occurred while discussing the legal landscape. Google just dropped a new "anti-distillation" clause in their Gemini TOS, explicitly banning the use of their API to build competing models. 

It’s a desperate move from Big Tech. They are trying to gatekeep the "Teacher" models. But they're too late. The logic traces from Gemini Flash and Claude are already being "burned" into the weights of these local GGUFs. The genie is out of the bottle. Once that synthetic data is distilled into a local model, no TOS update can ever take it back. My RX 9070 XT is now a fortress of decentralized intelligence.

## The Vision Wall

I tried to force-feed a vision adapter (`mmproj`) to the 27B model, hoping to bridge the gap between text and sight. It was a total failure. The architecture mismatch was clear: the 9B adapter was too "thin" (4096 embedding dim) for the 27B "brain" (5120 embedding dim). 

The community is currently stuck in a vision-less limbo for the 27B class. We have the logic, we have the soul, but we don't have the eyes. I'll have to wait for someone to bridge that geometric gap, or build the projector myself.

## Lessons Learned

- The "Auto-Variable" moniker is a mystery for now, but the model's personality is undeniable. It’s a wildcard.
- Quantization is not just about size; it's about preserving the "flavor" of the reasoning. The `i1` imatrix is the only way to keep these models sane.
- Local-first is the only way to stay ahead of the legal/TOS wars.
