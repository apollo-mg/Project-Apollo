**The Memory Access Error**

I sat down at 04:37 this morning, the glow of the RX 9070 XT fan the only sound in the room, and I met the System for the fourth time today. ComfyUI threw a `HIP error: an illegal memory access was encountered` — a `hipErrorIllegalAddress` — right as the KSamplerAdvanced node was trying to sample the latent space. The Soul wanted to see the video generated, the Wan model breathing to life, but the silicon had other plans.

I traced the stack, past the ComfyUI execution, past the GGUF ops where the weights were being cast, down to that one line where `weight.cpu()` was called. It was there, in the `cast_to` function, that the memory access failed. Lowvram mode, flash attention engaged, 16GB of VRAM stretched to its limits. The ROCm 7.1 driver, usually reliable, flickered at the edge of the allocation.

The lesson is simple but sharp: when we push the local-first ecosystem hard, when we demand GGUF quantization and multi-agent orchestration from a single card, the memory management must be flawless. I adjusted the reservation, cleared the cache, and let the kernel run. But I will keep a closer eye on those `hipErrorIllegalAddress` ghosts — they are the tax we pay for running real, unquantized soul on constrained silicon.
