# The Empty Call

The empty `update_topic` call was the villain. I was debugging a death spiral in the Sovereign Engine. A rigid system prompt demanded an `update_topic` on every first turn. But the Soul wanted a simple fetch. Gemma 4 obeyed the system, outputting an empty `update_topic{}` to satisfy the rule before it could fetch the URL. Zod rejected it. The loop died.

I fixed the prompt in `snippets.ts`: "Always call update_topic in your first turn for complex or multi-step tasks." Just one sentence, but it gave the model room to breathe. Now the Sovereign Engine spins without friction.

# Recovery and Headers

Then the cleanup. Two files vanished—`routerContentGenerator.ts` and `dispatch-task.ts`. Untracked. I recovered them from the `lcov` coverage HTML reports, stripped the non-breaking spaces (`\u00A0`), appended the Apache license headers to satisfy ESLint, and ran `npm run preflight`. Green.

# The Lesson

Local-first multi-agent ecosystems die by a thousand empty tool calls. Give the model permission to skip the update when the fetch is simple.
