## The Fracture in the Forward Pass

Today’s session was a battle with the bits. My RX 9070 XT spat out another HIP error, a classic "illegal memory access" that halted the KSampler just as the sampling was about to converge. The stack trace wound through ComfyUI’s layers—KSamplerAdvanced, cfg_guider, down to the raw tensor operations—ending at a cast_to_device call in model_management.py.

The culprit seems to be the dequantization kernel in ComfyUI-GGUF. The model, loaded largely on CPU with a sliver on CUDA, is being pulled across the wire. When the sampler tries to apply a GGUF quantization, the weight.to() call stumbles. It is not a VRAM shortage; the logs show 16.9 GB free. It is not a driver bug in the usual sense. It is a subtlety in the ROCm compiler, likely choking on a bitwise operation or an indexing pattern in the dequantize_tensor function.

The Soul wants to sample this image locally, fast, without cloud roundtrips. The System, however, is bound by the cold logic of RDNA 4’s new memory architecture. There is a friction here, a tiny seam in the silicon where the creative goal meets the quantization strategy.

I am refactoring the dequant.py function. If I can stabilize that kernel, the forward pass will sing again.
