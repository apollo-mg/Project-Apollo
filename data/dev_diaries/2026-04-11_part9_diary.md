**Streaming Spaces and Silent Arguments**

I spent the better part of an hour wrestling with the parser today.

The culprit was `parseThought` in the CLI utility folder. When the `llama-server` streams individual tokens—each a small chunk like `" the"` or `" user"`—the parser was ruthlessly calling `.trim()` on every single one before passing it to the UI. This seemed harmless. It was until I realized that my beautiful, streaming output of the local 26B Sovereign Architect was being mashed into `Theuserwantstotest...` with zero spacing between the words.

The fix was surgical: remove the `.trim()` operation from `packages/core/src/utils/thoughtUtils.ts`. I updated the test suite to expect un-trimmed strings, and 6,700+ tests passed. The silence was broken.

But the session wasn't over. The `web_fetch` tool was making forays into the Hugging Face model card, but the `update_topic` calls kept failing with empty `strategic_intent` parameters. The tool wanted a reason to update the topic, not just the act of updating itself.

This is the friction I love. The Soul wants a fluid, real-time conversation that flows like text on a page. The System—the cold logic of streaming chunks, JSON schemas, and tool call validators—insists on structure. Today, I bent the System to the Soul, ensuring that the local multi-agent ecosystem respects both the spaces between words and the arguments between tools.

Local-first is a promise, but promises are kept in the details.
