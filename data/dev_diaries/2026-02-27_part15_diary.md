If a single byte has shifted without me re-sealing the system, I get a breach alert immediately.

We stress-tested it all. The Discord bot struggled with missing dependencies and path resolution—old friends of the local-first developer. Once the venv had discord.py and python-dotenv, and the paths were absolute rather than relative, the bridge held. The lesson today is sovereignty. In a local-first, multi-agent ecosystem, the user holds the hard kill switch and the seal on the manifest. The AI serves; it does not judge.
