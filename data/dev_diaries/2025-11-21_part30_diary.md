# Dev Diary: 2025-11-21
## Part 30: The GELU Betrayal

The crash came at step zero.

Not even a warm pixel had rendered before the HIP error tore through the pipeline: *illegal memory access* in a GELU activation. Of course it did. Of course it would be the activation function.

---

### The Pattern Emerges

We'd already beaten RMSNorm into submission last week, wrapping it in FP32 armor when the inputs threatened to wander into FP16/BF16 death territory on this RDNA4 silicon. The Wan2.1-14B model had loaded—9.7GB of quantized weights, Q4_K_M, breathing down the 16GB of VRAM on my RX 9070 XT. It had survived dequantization. It had survived the RMSNorm layers.

Then it hit the feed-forward network.

```
File "/home/mark/gemini/ai/ComfyUI/comfy/ldm/wan/model.py", line 258, in forward
    y = self.ffn(ffn_input)
```

And inside that FFN, buried in a Sequential block:

```python
self.ffn = nn.Sequential(
    Linear(dim, ffn_dim),
    nn.GELU(approximate='tanh'),  # ← The executioner
    Linear(ffn_dim, dim)
)
```

The approximate tanh variant. The one that promises speed and delivers segfaults on certain AMD compute units when fed subnormal floats.

---

### The Operations Object

Here's where the architecture whispered its first clue.

RMSNorm wasn't hardcoded anywhere. It lived behind an `operations` object—a factory pattern, a strategy interface that the `WanModel` receives at initialization:

```python
operation_settings.get("operations").RMSNorm
```

That means the GELU could live there too. Or at least, a wrapper could. A safe activation that upcasts to FP32, computes, then casts back—sacrificing nanoseconds for stability.

The investigation traced the `operations` object back through `WanModel.__init__`, but the session ended before we found its birthplace. Some battles end in reconnaissance.

---

### The Hard Truth

This is the friction I live with:

**The Soul** wants sovereign intelligence—local-first, multi-agent ecosystems running on consumer hardware, dreaming in latent space without asking permission from cloud APIs.

**The System** is a 16GB RDNA4 GPU that treats certain FP16 operations like radioactive material.

The Wan2.1 model is bleeding edge. ROCm 7.1 is bleeding edge. PyTorch 2.8.0+rocm is bleeding edge. And bleeding edge means bleeding.

---

### The Lesson

Patterns matter more than patches.

We didn't just fix RMSNorm. We found the `operations` abstraction layer that could let us swap implementations without surgical strikes through every model file. That's the architecture win.

The GELU fix will be mechanical once we locate where the operations registry lives. But mechanical doesn't mean trivial—every wrapper adds overhead, every cast adds latency. The question becomes: how much friction can the system absorb before the vision buckles under its own implementation weight?

Tomorrow: tracing the operations object to its source, and building the wrapper that keeps Apollo dreaming in full precision.
