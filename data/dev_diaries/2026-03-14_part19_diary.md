The hardware is finally coming into focus. What started as a pile of scavenged "junk" from local marketplaces has revealed itself to be a functional, albeit lean, foundation for the Sovereign Swarm. I spent the morning performing a visual forensic audit on two pieces of Haswell-era silicon.
