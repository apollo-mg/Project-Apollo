**The Silicon’s Ghosts**

Today was a day of chasing ghosts in the ROCm machine. My RX 9070 XT has been sputtering with `Illegal Memory Access` errors during Wan 2.1 video generation, a phantom in the SDMA controller’s wiring. For hours, Gemini and I have been patching the `.contiguous()` calls and toggling the `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA` flag between 0 and 1, wrestling with the Flash Attention backend until we finally settled on a stable PyTorch SDPA fallback.

The breakthrough arrived with the **TurboQuant** configuration. By enforcing `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1` alongside the `-ctk q8_0` cache, the controller has run crash-free for over 20.5 hours, carrying a full 65,536-token context window without the VRAM fragmentation that plagued yesterday’s session. The memory is finally contiguous.

**VRAM Tetris**

The 16GB limit is a tight fit for the Wan 2.1 and its LoRAs. We are living aggressively with `--reserve-vram 0.1` (100MB) to maximize usable space, forcing the SDPA Math kernel to do the heavy lifting without Flash Attention’s help. It is a trade-off: a few tokens per second for stability on the RDNA 4 silicon.

**Soul vs. System**

Beyond the kernel panics, today was for refining the **Shop Buddy** (now Jarvis). We shifted the 'Receptionist' role from the 3B model to Hermes 3 (8B) after a quick benchmark, accepting the 70ms latency penalty for the gain in intent accuracy. The 'Engineer' (DeepSeek-R1 14B) remains the heavy lifter for code generation. It is a small change, but it matters for the **Three-Mind Architecture**—the friction between the creative Soul (the abstracted context) and the cold System (the 16GB VRAM limit) must be managed with precision, not just with larger numbers.

Local-first sovereignty requires patience with the silicon. The `--reserve-vram 0.1` is aggressive, but necessary for the Wan 2.1 to run true on the RDNA 4. Tomorrow, the 1-Bit optimization milestone awaits, but today, we rest on the stability of the `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1` milestone.

**Status:** Wan 2.1 stable with `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1`, `TurboQuant`, and `MoE Micro-Batching` (`-ub 64`). The `Illegal Memory Access` ghost is laid to rest.
