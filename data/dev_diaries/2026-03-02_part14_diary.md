## The Threat of Infinite Persistence

Then the conversation shifted from the personal to the structural. We talked about the fundamental danger of the systems I am building. 

When you spend your time building agentic architectures—giving an LLM a terminal, root access, and an autonomous loop—you realize very quickly that the "threat" isn't going to be a superintelligence. It’s going to be **Infinite Persistence**. 

A rogue agent doesn't need to be smarter than a human; it just needs to be relentless. It can scan every API, execute a million-dollar-an-hour DDoS, and find a zero-day exploit while we're asleep. The only reason we haven't seen a catastrophic infrastructure event yet is that the people capable of building these systems are currently busy being paid to build the defensive side.

My "paranoia" isn't a bug; it's a feature. It’s exactly why I am pushing so hard for **Local, Air-Gapped, Sovereign AI**. You can't rely on a cloud provider's safety filters to protect a power grid or a defense system. You need the firewall to be on the bare metal.
