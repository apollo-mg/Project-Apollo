## Timestamp: 2024-05-24 (Simulated)
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Objective
Identify CUDA-specific kernels and dependencies within ComfyUI custom nodes that prevent full utilization of the ROCm 7.10/2.10 stack, specifically looking for missing optimizations like `sageattention`.

### Hypothesis
The current environment is "successfully restored" but functionally throttled because custom nodes rely on pre-compiled CUDA binaries or specific CUDA-only libraries (e.g., `torch.cuda.amp` or custom C++ extensions) that do not have direct ROCm equivalents, forcing the system into a fallback mode that lacks peak efficiency.

### Plan
1. Locate the ComfyUI installation and its `custom_nodes` directory.
2. Scan `requirements.txt` and `pyproject.toml` files within custom nodes for CUDA-specific dependencies.
3. Search for imports of `torch.cuda` or calls to custom CUDA kernels in `.py` and `.cpp`/`.cu` files.
4. Identify nodes that claim to implement attention optimizations (like SageAttention) and check their implementation details.
