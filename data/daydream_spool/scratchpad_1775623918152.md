## Analysis
The Epiphany's claim of a 334.4W limit seems to be a snapshot of a specific state or a different configuration, as the current `power1_cap` is already at the VBIOS ceiling of 374W. However, the `power1_cap_default` is significantly lower at 304W. 

This confirms the "friction" mentioned: the system defaults to a lower power envelope (304W) than the hardware is capable of (374W). The 334.4W mentioned in the epiphany might have been a previous state or a specific kernel-imposed limit that has since been overridden or is currently being bypassed by a manual setting.

The core issue is the delta between `power1_cap_default` and `power1_cap_max`.

## Proposed Architectural Update: Hardware Capability Profile (HCP)
We need a mechanism to ensure the GPU always operates at its maximum capability defined by the VBIOS, rather than falling back to the lower `power1_cap_default` after reboots or driver updates.

### Proposed Implementation: `hw-capability-reconciler.service`
1. **Profile Definition:** A JSON/YAML file mapping hardware IDs to their VBIOS-derived limits.
2. **Monitoring:** A systemd service that runs on boot and periodically checks `power1_cap` against the profile.
3. **Reconciliation:** If `power1_cap` < `profile.max_limit`, the service attempts to write the `max_limit` to `power1_cap`.
