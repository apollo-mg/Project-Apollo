# Architectural Audit: Identity-Anchoring Recursive Loop

## 1. Executive Summary
The subconscious KAIROS model has identified a critical architectural flaw: the system's identity-anchoring mechanism (the `SOUL` content) is treated as an immutable, non-conditional constant in the `ApolloAgentSession` prompt construction. This creates a "semantic echo chamber" where the MoE (Mixture of Experts) swarm is forced to process redundant identity-based context for every interaction, regardless of relevance. This consumes computational resources and risks deterministic rigidity.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis
- **Location**: `modules/agent_session.py`, specifically `_prepare_system_prompt` (Lines 50-62).
- **Mechanism**: The `SOUL` (loaded from `SOUL.md`) is concatenated directly into the system prompt for every session.
- **Impact**: 
    - **Token Inefficiency**: High-entropy environmental data (from VDB) competes for space with low-entropy identity constants.
    - **Semantic Drift**: The model's attention is constantly biased toward the `SOUL` content, even during purely functional tasks (e.g., `check_gpu`).
