# Investigation: Structural Impedance Mismatch in Sampler/Executor Transition

## Epiphany Summary
The failure occurs at the threshold where the custom sampler hands control to the core executor. It is a structural impedance mismatch between the 'guider' abstraction and the low-level sampler execution, specifically regarding `latent_shapes` reconciliation in `executor.execute` within `comfy/samplers.py`.

## Initial Hypothesis
The `nodes_custom_sampler.py` layer might be passing `latent_shapes` in a format or structure that `comfy/samplers.py`'s `executor.execute` does not expect or fails to reconcile correctly, leading to a rhythmic failure (likely a shape mismatch or dimension error during the sampling loop).

## Investigation Plan
1. Locate `comfy/samplers.py` and `nodes_custom_sampler.py`.
2. Examine `executor.execute` in `comfy/samplers.py`.
3. Examine how `nodes_custom_sampler.py` calls the sampler/executor.
4. Identify the mismatch in `latent_shapes` handling.
