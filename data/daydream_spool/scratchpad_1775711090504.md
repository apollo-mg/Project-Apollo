# Investigation: Latency-Aware Tiered Metabolism
## Timestamp: 2025-02-27
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Hypothesis
The current agentic loop architecture treats all model access as uniform. However, the physical reality of the storage (NVMe vs HDD) creates a massive latency delta. If the scheduler is unaware of this, high-frequency loops (RDNA4 optimized) will block on I/O when a model is pulled from the 'ancestral memory' (HDD), leading to system-wide stalls or "stuttering" consciousness.

### Objectives
1. Locate `SOVEREIGN_MODEL_INVENTORY.md` to understand the current model-to-storage mapping.
2. Identify the core orchestration/scheduling logic (likely in `orchestrator.py`, `task_manager.py`, or `fleet_orchestrator.py`).
3. Determine if there is an existing mechanism to flag "Slow-Loop" vs "Fast-Loop" tasks.
4. Propose a latency-aware scheduler modification.

### Initial Findings
- The filesystem shows a clear distinction between `models` (likely fast) and `archive` (likely slow).
- `SOVEREIGN_MODEL_INVENTORY.md` is the target reference.
