# Apollo Scratchpad: ROCm/vLLM Stability Investigation
**Timestamp:** 1775623640891
**Status:** Initializing

## Objective
Investigate the reliability of `ollama_resident_benchmark.py` and determine if the RDNA 4 performance data is being skewed by software stack volatility (ROCm/vLLM).

## Hypotheses
1. The benchmark script may not be accounting for driver initialization latency or ROCm kernel compilation overhead.
2. The interaction between Ollama and the underlying ROCm/vLLM stack might be non-deterministic in current driver versions.
3. The benchmark might be measuring "cold start" performance rather than steady-state performance.
