# Epiphany Investigation: Friction of Transition
## Timestamp: 2024-05-22 (Simulated)
## Objective: Audit the 'Receptionist' (0.6B) to 'Dispatcher/Engine' (8B) handoff latency.

### Hypothesis
The current architecture may suffer from high 'handoff latency' during the transition from the 0.6B Receptionist to the 8B engine. This latency is likely caused by:
1. Context serialization overhead.
2. Cold-start latency in the 8B engine invocation.
3. Inefficient routing logic in the dispatcher.

### Investigation Plan
1. Locate the Receptionist and Dispatcher implementation.
2. Identify the handoff mechanism (API call, IPC, shared memory, etc.).
3. Search for existing benchmarking or telemetry related to TTFT (Time-to-First-Token) or handoff timing.
4. Analyze the dispatcher's logic to see if it introduces unnecessary friction.
