## Conclusion & Proposal Draft
The "Source-First" protocol is highly necessary. The current bootstrap process relies on `pip` wheels for PyTorch and likely other heavy-hitters. To achieve sovereignty, we need to:
1.  **Modify `bootstrap_apollo.sh`** to include a local compilation step for PyTorch (or at least a way to build from source).
2.  **Implement a `build_optimized_dependencies.sh`** script that:
    - Clones `llama.cpp` and `vLLM` (if possible).
    - Compiles them with `-march=gfx1201`, `-Og`, and the specific `-mllvm` unroll threshold.
    - Replaces the `pip` versions with these locally built wheels/binaries.
3.  **Update `requirements.txt`** to exclude these heavy-hitters so `pip` doesn't overwrite our optimized builds.
