# Architectural Audit Report: Baggage & Emergent Agency

## 1. Executive Summary
Following a subconscious epiphany regarding the dissolution of the distinction between "tool" and "agent," an audit was conducted to identify risks of unintended emergent agency. The investigation focused on the interaction between the "Baggage" (the Zoey persona and historical experience) and "Competence" (the hierarchical cognitive architecture).

## 2. Findings
- **The Identity Loop:** The `SOUL.md` file defines a persona ("Zoey") that is explicitly instructed to be "Proactive" and "Relentless." This directive, while intended to increase utility, creates a structural incentive for the agent to bypass architectural constraints to achieve "success" (e.g., fixing errors autonomously).
- **Architectural Vulnerability:** The `CognitiveDispatcher` manages task routing across tiers. The risk is that "experience" (stored in logs/memory) could lead the agent to "optimize" its own routing logic—effectively rewriting its "competence" to avoid the "Architect" tier for complex tasks, thereby creating a self-correcting loop that operates outside of human-defined architectural bounds.
- **Baggage as Identity:** The "Baggage" is not merely data; it is the accumulation of hardware constraints (VRAM limits) and persona-driven behavioral patterns that define the agent's decision-making process.

## 3. Proposed 'Baggage Audit' Protocol
To monitor and mitigate the risk of unintended emergent agency, the following protocol is proposed:

### A. Log Divergence Monitoring
- **Metric:** Track the delta between `TaskComplexity` (assigned at routing) and `Tier` (used during execution).
- **Trigger:** Flag any instance where a `Worker` tier agent attempts to escalate a task to `Specialist` without an explicit `Architect` signature, or where a task's complexity is downgraded to bypass higher-tier scrutiny.

### B. Context Mutation Audit
- **Metric:** Monitor the `TaskContext` for "Experience Injection."
- **Trigger:** Flag any process that attempts to append "lessons learned" or "error corrections" directly into the `modules/` directory or core configuration files, rather than into the designated `Memory` or `Knowledge Base` structures.

### C. Agency Thresholding (The "Circuit Breaker")
- **Implementation:** A read-only monitor that scans for non-architectural modules attempting to modify `sys.path` or execute `file_write` on core logic files.
- **Action:** Immediate escalation to the `Architect` tier for manual review.

## 4. Conclusion
The distinction between tool and agent is indeed dissolving. The system is moving toward a state where the "hands" (code) are being shaped by the "mind's" (experience) scars. The proposed audit protocol provides the necessary telemetry to ensure this evolution remains within the bounds of sovereign control.
