The "Epiphany" suggests a tension between the physical layer (drivers/venv) and the cognitive layer (documentation/GEMINI.md). The goal is to ensure that GEMINI.md serves as a bridge, preventing context loss during environment shifts (e.g., driver upgrades or venv rebuilds).

2.  **Assess Hardware/Substrate Documentation:** Is there a clear link between the ROCm/driver state and the agent's operational requirements?
3.  **Identify Gaps:** Where does the "bridge" fail?

- **Weaknesses/Gaps:**
