### Audit Findings

#### 1. The "Reflex" (Bash/Low-Level)
- `vram_management.py` contains a `smart_vram_guard` function. This is a "reflex" that triggers `unload_comfy_vram` when a threshold is hit.
- `system_monitor.py` provides raw hardware stats (CPU, RAM, Disk, Temp) but is essentially a passive data provider.
- The "reflex" is currently *reactive*: it waits for a threshold to be crossed before acting.

#### 2. The "Intellect" (Python/High-Level)
- `telemetry_layer.py` is an "Immutable Telemetry Layer" designed for state persistence. It records *what happened* (transitions, events) but does not *decide* what to do.
- `apollo.py` and `apollo_heartbeat.py` represent the core logic, but they seem to rely on external signals or manual triggers rather than an integrated feedback loop.

#### 3. The Gap (The "Loop of Survival")
- The system is currently in a state of **Reactive Survival**:
    - `vram_management.py` detects high VRAM $\rightarrow$ triggers cleanup $\rightarrow$ waits for release.
    - `system_monitor.py` provides data $\rightarrow$ likely used by some script to restart/stop.
- There is no **Homeostatic Controller**: A component that consumes `telemetry_layer.py` data and `system_monitor.py` stats to *predict* and *prevent* threshold breaches (e.g., "If VRAM is increasing at $X$ rate and we are entering a high-complexity sampling phase, offload *now* before the threshold is hit").

### Proposed Architectural Update: The Homeostatic Bridge

**Goal:** Move from "Threshold-Triggered Reflex" to "Predictive Homeostasis."

**Proposed Changes:**
1. **Integrate Telemetry into Decision Making:** Modify the `telemetry_layer.py` to not just log, but to provide a "Trend Analysis" API.
2. **Create a `HomeostasisController`:** A new module that:
    - Polls `system_monitor.py` and `vram_management.py`.
    - Analyzes trends (e.g., VRAM growth rate, temperature acceleration).
    - Communicates with the "Intellect" (LLM/Process Manager) to adjust workload *before* a crash/restart is required.
3. **Bridge the Reflex:** Instead of `smart_vram_guard` being a standalone check, it becomes a component of the `HomeostasisController`.
