## Refined Metric for "Thrash"
A "Thrash" event is defined as:
- `(SwapUsed / SwapTotal) > 0.5` (More than 50% swap usage)
- OR `(MemFree / MemTotal) < 0.05` (Less than 5% free RAM)
- AND `(SystemLoad) > Threshold` (Optional, but helpful)

This allows us to pinpoint the exact turn where the hardware "collapses".
