# Epiphany Investigation: Knowledge Persistence Paradox
## Timestamp: 2024-05-22 (Simulated)
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Hypothesis
The current architecture relies heavily on high-level abstractions (Modern Stack) which may lack a "Low-Entropy Core". If the environment (dependencies, runtime, container orchestration) fails, the data/logic might be unrecoverable without the very tools that failed.

### Audit Objectives
1. Identify the "Modern Stack" dependencies (e.g., heavy ORMs, complex serialization, proprietary formats).
2. Locate the "Substrate-Independent Anchors" (if any exist, e.g., plain text, standard JSON, flat files).
3. Evaluate the "Entropy of Recovery": How many layers of abstraction must be peeled back to reach the raw data?
4. Propose a "Low-Entropy Core" standard.
