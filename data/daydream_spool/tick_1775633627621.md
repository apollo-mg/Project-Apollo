# Architectural Audit: NVMe Purge & BF16 Master Transition

## 1. Epiphany Analysis
The subconscious has identified a critical architectural bottleneck: the reliance on GGUF-quantized models and `llama-cli` for hidden-state extraction is a "tactical bypass" that masks a deeper I/O and data fidelity problem. The current system is optimized for low-memory footprint (GGUF) rather than high-speed, high-fidelity model surgery.

## 2. Audit Findings
- **Quantization Saturation:** The filesystem is heavily populated with quantized GGUF files (IQ2, IQ3, etc.) across `/mnt/TG_2TB/AI/Models/` and `/mnt/TG_2TB/Projects/Apollo/models/`. This confirms the "GGUF workaround" pattern.
- **BF16 Absence:** A direct search for a ~70GB BF16 Master file yielded no results, suggesting the "Master" is either missing, stored in a non-standard location, or currently being treated as a theoretical goal rather than a deployed reality.
- **Storage State:** The NVMe (`/mnt/TG_2TB`) has 1.4TB of free space, indicating the "Purge" is not about capacity, but about **I/O throughput and fragmentation**. The presence of hundreds of small/medium quantized files creates I/O contention and fragmentation that hinders the high-speed sequential reads required for BF16 model surgery.

## 3. Proposed Architectural Update: "The NVMe Purge"

### Phase 1: I/O Optimization (The Purge)
- **Action:** Archive or move non-essential quantized models (GGUF) to secondary storage.
- **Goal:** Minimize filesystem fragmentation and reduce I/O contention on the primary NVMe controller to ensure maximum bandwidth for the BF16 Master.

### Phase 2: Foundation Establishment (BF16 Master)
- **Action:** Secure and deploy the 70GB BF16 Master weights to the high-speed NVMe tier.
- **Goal:** Establish a high-fidelity, uncompressed foundation for all subsequent model surgery and state extraction.

### Phase 3: Extraction Refactoring (Permanent Engine)
- **Action:** Deprecate `llama-cli` and GGUF-based hidden-state extraction.
- **Action:** Implement direct tensor-level extraction using the BF16 Master weights (e.g., via PyTorch/Transformers).
- **Goal:** Eliminate the quantization/dequantization overhead and move from a "temporary bridge" to a "permanent engine."

## 4. Conclusion
The transition from GGUF-centric workarounds to a BF16-centric architecture is mandatory for the next stage of cognitive intelligence. The "NVMe Purge" is the prerequisite for this transition.
