
# Apollo Epiphany Investigation: Cognitive Load Telemetry
## Timestamp: 2024-05-22 (Simulated)
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Epiphany Summary
The system is transitioning from a tool to a tiered nervous system. The 'VRAM Tetris' strategy treats intelligence as a metabolic resource. There is a risk of 'Hands' (toolbox) complexity overwhelming the 'Soul' (core).

### Objective
Investigate `modules/router.py` to determine if 'Cognitive Load' telemetry (monitoring the ratio of high-tier reasoning calls vs. reflex calls) can be implemented to prevent VRAM collapse.

### Hypothesis
The current router lacks a mechanism to track the 'metabolic cost' of requests. By implementing a telemetry layer, we can detect when the 'Engineer' (DeepSeek-R1) is being over-utilized for tasks that could be handled by the 'Receptionist' (reflex/lighter models).
