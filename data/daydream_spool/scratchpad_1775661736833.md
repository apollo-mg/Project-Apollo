# Epiphany Investigation: Salience-Based Expansion Protocol
## Timestamp: 2025-05-22 (Simulated)
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Hypothesis
The subconscious identifies a pattern of "cognitive looping" where high-weight information (like AWQ) is being reinforced through repetition rather than integration. The proposed solution is a "salience-based expansion protocol": using the detection of repeated high-weight nodes as a trigger to activate related, lower-weight architectural concepts.

### Investigation Plan
1. **Identify "Salient Weight" Mechanisms:** Search for where the system handles "salience," "weight," or "importance" in memory/context management.
2. **Identify "Cognitive Loops":** Look for logic that handles repetition or redundancy in memory/context.
3. **Audit Memory/Context Management:** Examine `Memory/`, `src/`, and `modules/` to see how context is scaled or pruned.
4. **Propose Implementation:** Draft a change to the context/memory management layer to implement the expansion protocol.

### Findings
1. **Memory System Structure:** `src/modules/memory_system.py` implements a three-tier system:
    - Tier 1: `Tier1WorkingBuffer` (LRU, volatile, immediate context).
    - Tier 2: `Tier2AssociativeCache` (SQLite, semantic search, short-term).
    - Tier 3: `Tier3LongTermKnowledge` (ChromaDB/File-based, long-term).
2. **Potential for Expansion Protocol:** The `TieredMemorySystem` (lines 232+) manages these tiers. The "salience-based expansion" can be implemented by monitoring access patterns or "weight" (metadata) in Tier 1/2 and triggering a "context expansion" (e.g., pulling related Tier 2/3 nodes into Tier 1) when a high-weight node is detected.
3. **Missing Link:** There is currently no explicit "salience" or "weight" field in `MemoryEntry` (line 25) that is used to trigger cross-tier activation. The `metadata` field is generic.

### Proposed Architectural Change
1. **Update `MemoryEntry`:** Add a `salience_score` field to `MemoryEntry`.
2. **Update `TieredMemorySystem`:** 
    - Implement a `detect_salience_loop` or similar mechanism.
    - When a `MemoryEntry` with a high `salience_score` is accessed in Tier 1, trigger a `semantic_search` in Tier 2/3 to find "related" nodes.
    - Aggressively promote these related nodes to Tier 1 to "scale the activation" of the context.
3. **Implementation Detail:** This prevents the "cognitive loop" by ensuring that instead of just seeing the same high-weight node, the system's context window is populated with its semantic neighbors.
