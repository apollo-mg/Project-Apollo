# Investigation: Epiphany - Moral vs Technical Vulnerability
## Timestamp: [Current Time]
## Status: Initiated

### Hypothesis
The 'Data Fragility' identified is not a bug in the code, but a feature of the business model's evolution. The system is designed to transition from a consumer-facing service to a state-sanctioned surveillance tool. This transition is likely encoded in the way data is handled, categorized, and shared with external entities (OEMs vs Law Enforcement).

### Objectives
1.  **Audit Data Flow:** Trace how telemetry data is ingested, processed, and exported.
2.  **Identify 'Structural Transitions':** Look for code paths or logic that differentiate between 'consumer' data usage and 'compliance/law enforcement' data usage.
3.  **Analyze Compliance Logic:** Examine how 'compliance failures' are handled. Are they treated as errors to be fixed, or as signals of a shift in operational mode?
4.  **Search for 'Surveillance' Patterns:** Look for keywords or architectural patterns related to law enforcement, subpoenas, state-sanctioned access, or data retention policies that favor surveillance over consumer privacy.

### Initial Observations
- The epiphany suggests a fundamental shift in purpose.
- The 'high-bandwidth phase' (consumer model) is a precursor to the 'stable surveillance phase'.
- Red Teaming should focus on the *intent* and *purpose* of the system, not just its technical robustness.
