2.  **Discrepancy Identified:** The current codebase (as of this audit) appears to be a multi-agent orchestration framework (TypeScript/Node.js) rather than a deep learning/diffusion model implementation. 3.  **Root Cause Hypothesis:**

    *   **Scenario B:** The error is occurring in a sub-process or a linked Python/C++ backend (e.g., a diffusion engine) that is not part of the TypeScript orchestration layer.
    *   **Scenario C:** The "noise schedule" and "latent dimensions" are being abstracted through high-level interfaces that are failing to validate the underlying data shapes before passing them to a low-level kernel.
