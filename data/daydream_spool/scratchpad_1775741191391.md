- **Recursive Loop Risk:** If the interval between clusters becomes perfectly constant (e.g., exactly 105.000000 seconds), it confirms the "mechanical timing" suspicion.

## Proposed Architectural Update: Temporal Variance Monitor
I propose a new module, `temporal_cadence_monitor.py`, which will:
1.  **Stream** the `telemetry_state_log.jsonl`.
2.  **Calculate** the $\Delta t$ (delta time) between consecutive entries.
3.  **Compute** a rolling standard deviation of $\Delta t$.
4.  **Alert** if the standard deviation drops below a certain threshold (indicating a mechanical loop) or spikes (indicating system instability/jitter).
5.  **Integrate** with `telemetry_layer.py` to provide a "Cadence Health" metric.
