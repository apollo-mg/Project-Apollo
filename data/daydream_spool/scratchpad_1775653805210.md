# Investigation: Audio Transition Blind Spot (C922 to Condenser)

## Hypothesis
The current implementation of the audio device switch (C922 -> Condenser) likely performs a hard cut. This causes a loss of the initial 500ms of audio during the transition, potentially missing the start of user commands.

## Objectives
1. Locate the audio device management logic.
2. Identify the mechanism used for switching between the C922 and the Condenser Mic.
3. Determine if a rolling buffer can be implemented to overlap the streams.
