### Analysis of `hardware_orchestrator.py`
- The current `HardwareOrchestrator` is heavily coupled to **HIP** (AMD's ROCm/HIP stack), as evidenced by the imports of `HIPKernelSynchronizationBarrier` and `HIPKernelBarrierContext`.
- This confirms the "tenant" status mentioned in the Epiphany: the orchestration layer is currently a consumer of the AMD ecosystem, not a hardware-agnostic architect.
- To implement the "Sovereign Engine" vision, we need a new abstraction layer that can switch between `HIP` (AMD), `CUDA` (NVIDIA), and a new `TT-Metal` (Tenstorrent) backend.
