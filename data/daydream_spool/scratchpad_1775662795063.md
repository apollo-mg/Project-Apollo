### Critical Discovery: Runtime Environment Variables
- **`start_architect.sh` (The Primary Engine):**
    - `export HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=0` (Directly confirms the Epiphany's mention of disabling SDMA).
    - `export HSA_XNACK=0`
    - `export GGML_HIP_FORCE_MMQ=1`
- **`start_coordinator_experimental.sh` (The Experimental/Performance Path):**
    - `export HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1` (This is the "Performance" path, which contradicts the "Integrity-First" paradigm).
- **`legacy_vault/infrastructure/launch_vllm_test.sh` (The Stability Path):**
    - `export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=0` (Disabling AITER, likely a high-performance/high-volatility feature).
- **`legacy_vault/infrastructure/apollo_vllm.sh` (The Performance Path):**
    - `export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1`
    - `export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MLA=1`
    - `export VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MHA=1`

### Conclusion of Audit
The Epiphany is correct. We currently have two distinct operational modes:
1.  **Integrity Mode (The "Sandbox"):** Found in `start_architect.sh`. It disables SDMA (`HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=0`) and uses conservative settings. This is the "defensive perimeter."
2.  **Performance Mode (The "Raw Hardware"):** Found in `start_coordinator_experimental.sh` and `apollo_vllm.sh`. It enables SDMA (`HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=1`) and enables AITER optimizations.

The "Cognitive Dissonance" and "RMS Norm" errors mentioned in logs are almost certainly the result of running in "Performance Mode" on RDNA 4 hardware.

### Final Proposal
Shift the default configuration for all generative video/large-scale inference tasks to the "Integrity Mode" settings. 
- **Mandatory Baseline:**
    - `HSA_ENABLE_SDMA=0`
    - `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=0`
    - `HSA_XNACK=0`
- **Action:** Update `start_coordinator_experimental.sh` to use the integrity settings by default, requiring an explicit `--performance` flag to enable the high-volatility features.
