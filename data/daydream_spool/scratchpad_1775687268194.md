## Hypothesis
The system is experiencing a type mismatch where tensors intended for indexing (discrete/Long) are being passed as floating-point values (continuous/Float), causing a failure in the KSampler or embedding pipeline.

## Investigation Plan

2. Search for tensor operations involving embeddings and samplers.
3. Look for potential casting issues (missing `.long()` calls).
4. Audit the KSampler node implementation.
