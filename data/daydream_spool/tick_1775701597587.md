## 2. Findings

$$Strength = Importance \times e^{(-\lambda \times days)} \times (1 + recall\_count \times 0.2)$$

### 2.2 The 'Core Belief' Safeguard

### 2.3 Identified Vulnerabilities
Despite the safeguard, the following risks were identified:
1. **Metadata Erasure:** The 'Daydream daemon' (the recall mechanism) must update the `recall_count`. If this update is implemented as a full metadata overwrite rather than a targeted patch, the `type: "core_belief"` tag may be lost, rendering the memory vulnerable to the Reaper.
2. **Semantic Decay (The "Noise" Problem):** Even if not deleted, a low `strength` value makes a memory statistically insignificant in vector space. If the `recall_count` does not outpace the $\lambda_{eff}$ decay, the 'core_belief' becomes "noise"—effectively lost to the system's intelligence.
3. **Incomplete Protection:** The current safeguard is localized to `prune_memories.py`. Other potential pruning or compaction processes (e.g., vector DB maintenance) may not respect this specific metadata tag.
