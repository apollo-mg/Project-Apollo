- **Email Ingestion Analysis:** `vllm_email_ingest.py` confirms the process. It uses a VLLM model (`Qwen2.5-7B-FP8`) to analyze email batches, extracting `topic`, `entities`, and `action`. The results are then injected into a vector database (`vdb`).

- **Architectural Blueprint for 'Feasibility Engine':**
    1. **Input A (DesignMind):** A CAD model or design specification (represented by the `cad_training.jsonl` data patterns).
    2. **Input B (Procurement Mind/Real-World):** The vector database populated by `vllm_email_ingest.py`, containing real-world constraints (lead times, stock issues, vendor changes).
    3. **The Engine (FeasibilityEngine):** A new module that performs a semantic cross-check. It takes a design concept, queries the vector store for related entities/parts, and evaluates if the "Real-World Constraint" (from emails) conflicts with the "Design Intent."
    4. **Output:** A "Feasibility Score" or a "Constraint Report" that informs the DesignMind to adjust the design before it reaches the procurement stage.
