The "Purge" likely refers to clearing out the massive collection of quantized models to ensure the BF16 Master has contiguous, high-speed access to the NVMe controller.
- **Hidden State Extraction:** The grep results show references to "Master Action Plan" and "Sovereign Entity Architecture," but no direct code implementing `llama-cli` for hidden-state extraction was found in the top-level search. This suggests the "bridge" might be in a script or a tool not yet indexed or located in a non-standard path.
