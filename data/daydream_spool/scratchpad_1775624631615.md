### Conclusion & Hypothesis
The "software bridge" is the abstraction layer between this orchestration framework and the vLLM inference engine. The "functional compromise" is the decision to run in "Eager Mode" (likely bypassing graph capture/optimization) to avoid the instability caused by the mismatch between vLLM's optimization attempts and the RDNA 4 Wave32 execution model. 

The 20% performance loss is the cost of stability.

### Final Proposal
1.  **Official Baseline:** Formally document "Eager Mode" as the stable baseline for RDNA 4 deployments in the project documentation.
2.  **Optimization Freeze:** Implement a safeguard in the orchestration layer (or a configuration flag) that explicitly disables graph capture and automated optimization when RDNA 4 hardware is detected, until vLLM compatibility is verified.
