# Architectural Audit & Proposal: MoE/Unsloth Synergy for RDNA 4

## 1. Executive Summary
The subconscious "Epiphany" identifies a critical opportunity to bridge the gap between frontier-scale intelligence and local RDNA 4 hardware. Current investigations confirm that while the hardware (RX 9070 XT) and the software stack (ROCm 7.2.0-2) are stable, the current model registry and benchmarking protocols are optimized for dense architectures (Gemma-4), leaving the potential of MoE (Mixture of Experts) architectures like MiniMax 2.5 and Qwen 3.5 untapped.

## 2. Audit Findings

### 2.1 Model Registry Status
- **Status:** **CRITICAL MISSING.**
- **Details:** `model_manifest.json` contains only Kokoro TTS models. MiniMax 2.5 and Qwen 3.5 variants are not registered.
- **Action Required:** Immediate ingestion and manifest update.

### 2.2 Hardware & Driver Environment
- **Current State:** ROCm 7.2.0-2 (Stable).
- **Risk Assessment:** The ROCm 7.11 "TheRock" preview is currently unsuitable for production due to `llama.cpp` regressions and Llama 3.1 validation failures.
- **Opportunity:** The synergy between MoE routing and Triton kernels may provide a way to optimize memory mapping, potentially bypassing some of the current driver-level inefficiencies observed in standard dense model implementations.

### 2.3 Benchmarking Baseline
- **Current Baseline:** `moe_shootout_results.json` only contains data for `Gemma-4` (Dense).
- **Gap:** There is no existing metric for VRAM-to-TPS efficiency for MoE models on the current RDNA 4 stack.

### 2.4 Optimization Status
- **Triton Usage:** Triton is present via PyTorch Inductor (as evidenced by `vllm_results_fp8_redhat.log`), but there is no evidence of specialized Unsloth-style kernel optimizations being utilized.

## 3. Proposed Architectural Update

### Phase 1: Ingestion & Registration
- **Task:** Retrieve MiniMax 2.5 and Qwen 3.5 variants.
- **Task:** Update `model_manifest.json` with appropriate metadata (type: moe, architecture: minimax/qwen).

### Phase 2: The "MoE-Triton" Benchmark Suite
Develop a new benchmarking module `scripts/moe_efficiency_benchmark.py` to quantify the "Epiphany" hypothesis.

**Benchmark Parameters:**
1. **Model Variants:** 
   - MiniMax 2.5 (MoE)
   - Qwen 3.5 (MoE)
   - Gemma-4 (Dense - Control Group)
2. **Execution Engines:**
   - Standard `vLLM` (HIP/ROCm).
   - `Unsloth`-optimized Triton kernels (Experimental).
3. **Primary Metrics:**
   - **VRAM Efficiency:** (Total Parameters / Peak VRAM Usage).
   - **Throughput:** Tokens Per Second (TPS) during prefill and decode.
   - **Routing Latency:** Time spent in MoE expert selection vs. compute.

### Phase 3: Kernel Optimization Research
- Investigate the feasibility of compiling Unsloth's Triton kernels specifically for the `gfx120x` target to maximize the RDNA 4 compute units.

## 4. Conclusion
The hypothesis is architecturally sound. The current bottleneck is not the model size, but the efficiency of the execution path. By prioritizing MoE models and specialized Triton kernels, we can effectively "expand" the perceived VRAM capacity of the local RDNA 4 hardware.
