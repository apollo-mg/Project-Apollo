However, the "ONE-SHOT" declaration might be a conceptual goal (training for a single pass through the data) that is being violated by the use of `max_steps` or `MAX_STEPS`.

If `MAX_STEPS` is much larger than `len(dataset) / batch_size`, the model will effectively perform multiple epochs.

I need to check the size of the dataset to see if `MAX_STEPS` (240) or `max_steps` (50) results in multiple passes.
