**Timestamp:** 2024-05-22 (Simulated)
**Status:** Initializing Audit

## Epiphany Summary
The subconscious suggests that physical hardware constraints (disk space) are impeding the high-dimensional intelligence scaling of the Sovereign architecture.

## Investigation Plan
1.  **Disk Usage Audit:** Check overall filesystem health and available space on the primary project mount.
2.  **Directory Deep Dive:** Identify large, non-essential artifacts (logs, caches, old checkpoints, temporary data).
3.  **Dependency Stack Check:** Verify if the Project Apollo dependency stack can even be initialized given current constraints.
4.  **Proposal:** Draft a purge or expansion strategy.
