## Technical Audit of `memory_core.py`

### Identified Flaw
The `perform_compaction_flush` method (lines 55-105) confirms the Epiphany's warning. 

1.  **Lossy Compression:** The `summary_prompt` (line 59) explicitly asks for: *"Summarize the key decisions, architectural facts, and context from our conversation so far. Output only the factual summary."*
2.  **Lack of Contextual Anchors:** The prompt instructs the model to output *only* the factual summary. This directly strips away the "why" (the reasoning, the surrounding state, the intent) that the Architect needs to prevent Contextual Drift.
3.  **Metadata Deficiency:** The metadata added to the `Document` (lines 75-82) only includes `source`, `type`, and `timestamp`. It does not capture the "state" or "intent" of the session at the time of the flush.
4.  **The "Liara" Effect:** The `surgeon-heretic:latest` model is being used to perform a highly reductive task. By forcing it to be "only factual," we are effectively deleting the nuance required for the long-term cortex (The Architect) to maintain structural integrity.

### Proposed Architectural Change
The `summary_prompt` must be re-engineered to include a requirement for "Contextual Anchors." Instead of just facts, it must capture the *rationale* and *environmental state*.

**New Prompt Strategy:**
- Request "Contextual Anchors": Explicitly ask for the *reasoning* behind decisions and the *state* of the system.
- Structured Output: Instead of a raw string, request a structured format (e.g., JSON or a specific Markdown schema) that separates `facts`, `decisions`, `rationale`, and `environmental_state`.
