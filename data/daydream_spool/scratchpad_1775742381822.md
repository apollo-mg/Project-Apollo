# Epiphany Investigation: GGUF Tensor State Transition Friction
## Timestamp: 2025-05-22 (Simulated)
## Status: Initializing Audit

### Hypothesis
The 'illegal memory access' error is likely caused by dynamic casting or tensor movement within the inference loop. The GGUF implementation might be attempting to re-cast or move tensors while a HIP kernel is executing, or the abstraction layer is losing pointer validity.

### Objective
1. Locate `ops.py` and the GGUF loading logic.
2. Identify where tensor casting/movement occurs during the inference loop.
3. Propose a shift to pre-casting weights during the loading phase.
