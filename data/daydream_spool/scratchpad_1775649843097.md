## Vulnerability Assessment:
The `architect` and `turbo` profiles are the primary 'completers'. The `turbo` profile, specifically using `turbo3` KV caching, is optimized for sequence rhythm/speed, making it the most susceptible to the 'drift' where the model prioritizes the next-token prediction pattern over factual content accuracy.

## Proposed Architectural Update:
1. **Implement `modules/pvl_engine.py` (Prior-Validation Layer):**
    - This engine will act as the 'Constructor' stabilizer.
    - It will perform a secondary, non-predictive check on high-stakes outputs.
    - For `architect` and `turbo` outputs, it will use a deterministic schema validator or a lightweight, non-autoregressive model (like a small BERT-based classifier or a rule-based parser) to verify structural integrity before the output is accepted by the `orchestrator.py`.
2. **Integrate PVL into `llm_interface.py`:**
    - Modify `llm_interface.py` to route outputs through the `pvl_engine.py` when the `DeploymentConfig` indicates a high-risk context.
3. **Update `orchestrator.py`:**
    - Add a `validation_layer` field to the `profiles.json` schema to specify which validation protocol to use for each profile.
