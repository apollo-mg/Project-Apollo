# Architectural Proposal: Low-Wattage Inference Stack (L-WIS)

## 1. Executive Summary
In response to the "Energy Sovereignty" epiphany, this proposal outlines the transition from a general-purpose inference stack to a "Low-Wattage" optimized stack. The goal is to maximize "Intelligence-per-Watt" (IpW) to ensure cognitive continuity during energy rationing or geopolitical constraints.

## 2. Core Objectives
- **Telemetry:** Implement real-time power consumption monitoring (Watts/Joules) to measure the energy cost of inference.
- **Quantization:** Shift primary inference workloads to extreme quantization (4-bit GGUF/K-quants or lower) to reduce memory bandwidth and compute energy.
- **Kernel Optimization:** Implement RDNA 4-specific compiler optimizations to mitigate known ROCm regressions.
