### Findings: Librarian Ingest Analysis
`librarian_ingest.py` confirms the "Library Model". It is a straightforward ingestion engine that:
1. Processes PDFs from a `COLD_VAULT_DIR`.
2. Fetches and extracts content from URLs via `trafilatura`.
3. Splits content into chunks and adds them to a vector store.

**Critique:**
- There is **zero** filtering logic. It ingests whatever is provided (PDF or URL).
- It lacks any concept of "relevance" or "utility" before ingestion.
- It is purely a volume-based ingestion tool.
