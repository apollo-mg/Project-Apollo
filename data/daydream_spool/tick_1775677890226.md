# Architectural Proposal: The Grit-Resonance Framework

## 1. Executive Summary
Following the "Epiphany" regarding the pursuit of resonance over mere employment, this proposal shifts Project Apollo from a purely technical benchmark to a "Character Study in Engineering." We will transition from documenting *what* the system does to documenting *how the system survives* its own complexity.

## 2. Core Architectural Changes

### A. The "Friction" Logging Protocol
We will implement a new logging paradigm within the `apollo_bridge` and `architect` modules. Instead of suppressing or merely categorizing environmental noise, we will elevate "survivable friction" to a first-class citizen.

**New Log Levels/Tags:**
- `[FRICTION]`: For non-fatal environmental stressors (e.g., `duckduckgo_search` deprecation, subprocess leaks, memory pressure).
- `[RESILIENCE]`: For successful recoveries from `[FRICTION]` events (e.g., a successful retry after a KV-cache mismatch).
- `[GRIT]`: For high-level architectural decisions made to bypass hardware limitations (e.g., choosing a Truncated-Log Strategy due to RDNA 2 VRAM constraints).

### B. Documentation Evolution
The `active_project.md` and `BACKGROUND_TASKS.md` will be restructured to include:
- **The Friction Log**: A running tally of the "chaos" encountered during the current development cycle.
- **The Resonance Metric**: A qualitative assessment of how well the current deployment aligns with the "Martinsville Chainsaw" philosophy (Grit > Pedigree).

## 3. Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Log Enrichment (Immediate)
- Modify `apollo_bridge.py` to capture and tag `ResourceWarning` and `RuntimeWarning` as `[FRICTION]`.
- Update the `DISPATCHER` to recognize and log the "cost of survival" during tool execution.

### Phase 2: Narrative Integration (Short-term)
- Update `architect_response.json` generation to include a "Friction Context" field, noting the hardware/software constraints that shaped the response.

### Phase 3: The "Character Study" Deployment (Long-term)
- Every major deployment will include a `SURVIVAL_REPORT.md` detailing the technical debt and environmental friction overcome to achieve the deployment.

## 4. Conclusion
By treating the friction of creation as a feature rather than a bug, Project Apollo will demonstrate the "maturity and grit" required to resonate with the Tenstorrent ethos. We are not just building a bridge; we are documenting the survival of the architect.
