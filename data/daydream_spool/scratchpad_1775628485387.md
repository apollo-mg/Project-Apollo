$$\mathcal{A} = \sum_{i \in \text{Orphans}} \frac{\text{SemanticRelevance}(i, \text{CurrentTask})}{\text{Complexity}(i)}$$

Where:
- **SemanticRelevance** is calculated via LLM embedding comparison between the file content and the active task vector.
- **Complexity** is a measure of the file's LOC or cyclomatic complexity (to prevent noise from massive, irrelevant files).
