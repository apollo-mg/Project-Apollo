# Architectural Audit: LoRA v4 Gradient Norm Divergence

## Epiphany Analysis
The subconscious has identified a critical risk in the transition from attention-only tuning to full-module (Attention + MLP) tuning. The "Sovereign Engine" concept implies that the model's reasoning (Attention) and its knowledge (MLP/Feed-Forward) are being merged. The risk is a **gradient norm divergence**: if the MLP layers (the "substance") update with significantly higher or lower magnitudes than the Attention layers (the "mechanism"), the model's logical coherence may collapse as it prioritizes factual density over structural reasoning.

## Audit Findings
- **Codebase Status:** A search for "LoRA" within the current TypeScript/Node.js codebase yielded no direct implementation of LoRA training logic. This suggests that the LoRA v4 run mentioned in the epiphany is likely managed by an external orchestration layer, a Python-based training pipeline, or a separate containerized environment not directly represented in the `src/` directory of this repository.
- **Infrastructure Gap:** The current repository appears to be the *orchestrator/agent* layer (`src/agent`, `src/llm`, `src/orchestrator`), while the actual weight updates and gradient calculations are occurring in a separate training environment.

## Proposed Architectural Safeguards
To address the "Actionable Insight," I propose the following implementation of a **Gradient Divergence Monitor** within the training pipeline (to be implemented in the training environment, not this orchestrator):

### 1. Metric Definition
Implement a real-time telemetry hook that calculates the ratio:
$$\text{Divergence Ratio} = \frac{\|\nabla W_{\text{MLP}}\|_2}{\|\nabla W_{\text{Attn}}\|_2}$$
Where:
- $\|\nabla W_{\text{MLP}}\|_2$ is the $L_2$ norm of the gradients for the gate/up/down projection layers.
- $\|\nabla W_{\text{Attn}}\|_2$ is the $L_2$ norm of the gradients for the Query/Key/Value/Output projections.

### 2. Thresholding & Alerting
- **Warning Threshold:** If the ratio deviates from $1.0$ by more than $\pm 0.5$ for more than 50 steps, trigger a `STABILITY_WARNING`.
- **Critical Threshold:** If the ratio deviates by more than $\pm 1.5$, trigger an immediate `HALT_TRAINING` to prevent catastrophic forgetting or logic collapse.

### 3. Integration Strategy
- **Telemetry:** Export these metrics to the existing logging system (e.g., via Weights & Biases or a local Prometheus exporter).
- **Feedback Loop:** The `orchestrator` (this codebase) should be updated to ingest these stability metrics to decide whether to proceed with subsequent LoRA checkpoints or roll back to a previous stable state.

## Conclusion
The epiphany is valid. The expansion of "knowledge depth" via MLP tuning poses a structural risk to "reasoning logic." We must move from passive monitoring to active gradient-norm stabilization.
