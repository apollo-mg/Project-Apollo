# Architectural Audit: The Structural Inversion (SDXL $\rightarrow$ Flux 2)

## 1. Executive Summary
The subconscious epiphany identifies a fundamental shift in generative AI: the transition from independent diffusion models to VLM-driven "embodied reasoning engines." Our current architecture, while sophisticated in its hierarchical task routing (`cognitive_dispatcher.py`), remains tethered to the "Descriptive Prompting" paradigm. We are currently using the VLM to *describe* the world to the "body" (the renderer/CAD engine), rather than using the VLM's internal world model to *dictate the physics* of the scene via latent or parametric constraints.

## 2. Current State Analysis
- **Cognitive Routing:** `modules/cognitive_dispatcher.py` successfully implements a tiered reasoning structure (Architect vs. Worker). However, the output of these tiers is primarily text-based instructions.
- **Visual Feedback Loop:** `modules/design_mind.py` utilizes vision (`desktop_eyes.py`) to provide context, but the interaction is purely descriptive (e.g., "Identify current tool/mode"). This is the "descriptive prompt engineering" identified as a legacy bottleneck.
- **Safety/Validation:** `modules/pvl_engine.py` provides a reactive safety layer against hallucinations but does not proactively integrate the VLM's world model into the generative process.
- **Data Synthesis:** `modules/synthetic_forge.py` creates training pairs (Plan $\rightarrow$ Code), which is a step toward reasoning, but it still treats the "Plan" as a linguistic precursor to "Syntax" rather than a latent semantic representation.

## 3. Identified Architectural Gap
The "Brain" (VLM) and the "Body" (Renderer/CAD Engine) are currently decoupled via a linguistic interface. To evolve into an "Embodied Reasoning Engine," we must bridge the gap between the VLM's high-level semantic understanding and the low-level physics/geometry of the output.

## 4. Proposed Architectural Evolution

### Phase 1: From Description to Parametric Constraint
**Objective:** Replace descriptive instructions with structured semantic deltas.
- **Change:** Modify `modules/design_mind.py` and the `Architect` tier to output structured JSON/FeatureScript deltas instead of natural language descriptions.
- **Mechanism:** The VLM should reason about the *geometric constraints* (e.g., "Coincident constraint between Point A and Point B") and output the specific parametric change required, rather than saying "The sketch is over-constrained."

### Phase 2: The Latent Semantic Bridge
**Objective:** Implement a direct mapping between VLM reasoning and the generative manifold.
- **New Module:** `modules/semantic_manifold_bridge.py`.
- **Function:** This module will translate the VLM's "world model" (its understanding of spatial relationships, physics, and causality) into a format that the "Body" can ingest directly (e.g., latent vectors for diffusion or parametric trees for CAD).

### Phase 3: Proactive Physical Validation
**Objective:** Evolve the `pvl_engine.py` into a "Physical Consistency Layer."
- **Change:** Shift `PriorValidationLayer` from detecting linguistic hallucinations to detecting *physical/geometric impossibilities* in the proposed latent/parametric deltas before they are rendered.

## 5. Conclusion
The shift from SDXL to Flux 2 is not just a model upgrade; it is a paradigm shift. Our architecture must move from "Text $\rightarrow$ Image" to "Reasoning $\rightarrow$ Physics." We are no longer building generators; we are building engines that reason through the medium of pixels and geometry.
