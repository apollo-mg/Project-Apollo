### Hypothesis

This "safety" mechanism causes massive PCIe overhead during the forward pass, which is being masked as "normal" execution.

### Investigation Plan

3.  **Audit Telemetry/Logging:** Check if there is any existing telemetry that tracks device transfers or device-to-host synchronization.
4.  **Propose "Strict" Mode:** Design a mechanism to replace these fallbacks with hard errors.
