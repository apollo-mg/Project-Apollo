# Investigation: Recursive Self-Measurement Loop
## Epiphany Analysis
The subconscious suggests that `get_free_memory` calls might be causing a recursive cycle or a bottleneck. This implies that the system's attempt to monitor its own resource availability is consuming the very resources it is trying to monitor, potentially leading to instability or race conditions.

## Initial Hypothesis
1.  **High Frequency:** `get_free_memory` is being called too frequently within a tight loop.
2.  **Resource Contention:** The call itself (likely involving system calls or reading `/proc/meminfo`) is expensive enough to trigger the instability it's trying to detect.
3.  **Lack of Throttling:** There is no caching or cooldown period between memory checks.

## Investigation Plan
1.  Search the codebase for all occurrences of `get_free_memory`.
2.  Analyze the call sites to determine frequency and context.
3.  Check for any existing caching mechanisms or lack thereof.
4.  Identify if these calls are part of a monitoring loop or a critical execution path.
