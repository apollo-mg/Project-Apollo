### 3. Proposed Implementation: `PredictiveKernelTracker`

#### A. Core Logic
The new module will implement a velocity-based estimation model:
1. **Kernel Velocity ($V_k$):** Monitor the frequency of commits to `llama.cpp/ggml/src` and `llama.cpp/ggml/include` that contain keywords: `[ROCm, HIP, SIMD, AVX, CUDA, kernel, optimized]`.
2. **Feature Complexity Weight ($C_f$):** A heuristic weight assigned to specific feature sets (e.g., "KV Cache Optimization" = 0.8, "New ROCm Kernel" = 1.2).
3. **Estimated Deployment Window ($T_{deploy}$):** 
   $$T_{deploy} = \frac{C_f}{V_k}$$
