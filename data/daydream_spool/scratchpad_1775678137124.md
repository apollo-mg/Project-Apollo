# Investigation Log: Dataset Fragmentation & Manual Patching
## Timestamp: 2025-05-22 (Simulated)
## Epiphany: 'vocab.txt' manual copying indicates a lack of cohesive dataset packaging.

### Initial Observations
- The epiphany suggests that `vocab.txt` is being manually moved/copied to training directories.
- This implies the training pipeline lacks a "bundling" step.
- Target dataset: `mark_f5`.

### Hypothesis
- Training scripts or orchestration layers (likely in `ai/` or `bin/`) assume the presence of certain files in the working directory.
- There is no unified `DatasetPackager` or `DatasetManifest` system.
- The `mark_f5` dataset structure is likely decoupled from its tokenizer requirements.

### Plan
1. Search for references to `vocab.txt` in the codebase to see how it is currently handled.
2. Search for `mark_f5` to understand its current storage and usage.
3. Identify the training orchestration logic.
4. Propose a `DatasetPackager` utility.
### Findings Update
- `mark_f5` and `vocab.txt` are not explicitly present in the current file tree (excluding `venv` and `hf_cache`).
- This confirms the "Epiphany": the files are likely transient or part of a dataset that is not currently "mounted" or "indexed" in the main project structure, reinforcing the idea that they are being manually injected into training environments.
- The absence of a `DatasetPackager` or similar utility in the `legacy_vault` or `ai/` directories suggests a structural gap.
- The training pipeline likely relies on external data directories that are not part of the version-controlled codebase.

### Proposed Solution: `DatasetPackager`
A new utility `ai/utils/dataset_packager.py` should be implemented to:
1. Accept a `dataset_id` (e.g., `mark_f5`).
2. Locate the source dataset files.
3. Identify required tokenizer files (e.g., `vocab.txt`, `tokenizer.json`, `tokenizer_config.json`).
4. Bundle these into a single, verifiable directory structure.
5. Generate a `manifest.json` containing metadata (version, checksums, dependencies).
6. Provide a CLI interface for the training orchestration layer to call before training starts.
