# Epiphany Investigation: T5 XXL Quantization vs. Latent Space Interface
## Status: Initializing Audit

**Hypothesis:** The semantic weight of the fp8-quantized T5 XXL encoder is being lost or misaligned during the transition to the latent space initialization. The 'EmptyLatent' might be receiving improperly scaled or incorrectly formatted embeddings, preventing the model from translating text semantics into structural latents.

**Investigation Plan:**
1. Locate the Wan loader and T5 XXL quantization logic.
2. Identify the interface where T5 embeddings are passed to the latent space/diffusion process.
3. Check for scaling factors, normalization, or precision mismatches (e.g., fp8 to fp16/bf16 conversion issues).
4. Audit the 'EmptyLatent' initialization to ensure it matches the expected dimensions and distribution for the Wan architecture.

**Current Focus:** Locating the relevant codebase for Wan and T5.
