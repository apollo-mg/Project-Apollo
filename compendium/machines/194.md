# .194: the four-P100 test host

Four Tesla P100 (16 GB, sm_60), dual socket. It is powered off between runs. How to share it, which ports are for
tests and how to power it are rules, in `AGENTS.md`; what is being served anywhere is in `STATUS.md`. This page holds
what was measured on the box. Line rules: `compendium/README.md`.

## Memory

- **128 GB of RAM (121.5 GiB visible to the OS), 64 GB of VRAM across the four P100s.** The 60 GiB in the August
  Flash-Next receipts predates the DIMM upgrade of about 2026-09-19. (measured 2026-11-20 at boot, `free`;
  `data/receipts/pi-thinking/sentence/sentence.log`)
- **sshd can reset connections for a few seconds after it first answers at boot,** so a driver that powers .194 on
  must wait for several consecutive answered commands before its first real check. (measured 2026-11-19;
  `data/receipts/pi-thinking/RESULT_SIZING.md`)

## Power

- **A cold boot takes 216 s from power-on to SSH, and the box idles at 218 to 226 W** with no model loaded (about 109 W
  of that is the four idle cards). That is the cost of leaving it on, and the reason runs end with a power-off.
  (measured 2026-09-03; `data/receipts/power-management/RESULT_194_POWER_CYCLE.md`)
- **The first power-on call to the BMC can fail and the box still comes up.** One run saw "Unable to establish IPMI v2
  / RMCP+ session" on the first call; SSH answered after 231 s. Check SSH, not the call's exit code. (measured
  2026-11-11; `data/receipts/abstend/qwen3.5-9b-kite2e/RESULT_KIT_ACCEPTANCE.md`)
- **The efficiency clock pin (1063 MHz) costs speculative decoding more than plain decoding.** Fully resident
  Flash-Next Q2 with MTP: 28.0 tok/s pinned, 34.1 at 1328 MHz (+22%; plain decode gains +11%). At 1328 MHz the 150 W
  cap starts clipping (per-card peaks 152 to 164 W). Record the clock with every speed number. (measured 2026-09-28;
  `data/receipts/qwen4exp/RESULT_FLASHNEXT_MTP_CLOCK.md`)

## Splitting a model across the cards

- **Always set `GGML_CUDA_ALLREDUCE=internal` for `-sm tensor`.** Left unset, this box picks NCCL, and NCCL fails on its
  P100s (a hard abort on buun master). (measured 2026-08-18 and 2026-09-02;
  `data/receipts/kv-tensor-split/RESULT_OWNERSHIP.md`, `data/receipts/buun-qwen4-pascal/RESULT_BUUN_MASTER_SM60.md`)
- **Tensor split wins prefill and loses single-stream decode** (Flash-Next, 4 cards): prefill 287 against 132 tok/s at
  8k with the default ubatch, 430 at `-ub 4096`; decode 14.3 against 21.1 tok/s. Layer split at `-ub` 2048 or more ran
  out of memory on 8k under `-ts 1,1,1,0.6`. (measured 2026-10-02;
  `data/receipts/qwen4exp/split-conc/RESULT_NUMA_PREFILL.md`)
- **With an explicit `-np` above 2, pass `--kv-unified`.** Without it two concurrent requests on non-adjacent slots
  decoded at 0.60x (10.3 against 17.3 tok/s per stream, Flash-Next layer split); it looked like a bistable box and was
  not NUMA. (measured 2026-10-02; `data/receipts/qwen4exp/split-conc/RESULT_NUMA_PREFILL.md`)

## Lanes and what a run costs

- **Two 27B lanes (cards 0+1 and 2+3) are bit-identical instruments.** The 23-arm quantization study ran one build on
  both lanes. (measured 2026-09-27; `data/receipts/quant-abstention/RESULT_MAIN.md`)
- **A 9B at Q8_0 fits one card** (9,017 MiB on GPU 0 alone), so four small-model lanes are possible. Staging weights on
  the host's disk and re-hashing there reproduced a hand run byte for byte, 240 of 240. (measured 2026-11-11;
  `data/receipts/abstend/qwen3.5-9b-kite2e/RESULT_KIT_ACCEPTANCE.md`)
- **Agent episodes on a 27B Q8_0 lane are slow.** Median 1.9 minutes for a five-call task; 18 to 31 minutes for a
  30-step episode, and one model took 45. Budget agent runs in lane-hours. (measured 2026-11-09;
  `data/receipts/agentic-gaming/RESULT_PILOT_v0.md`, `data/receipts/agentic-gaming/DESIGN_v1.md`)
